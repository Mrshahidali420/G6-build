## Key SERP patterns

- **No single wiki monopoly**: unlike most gaming niches, GTA.fandom.com does NOT dominate every query — it shows up mainly on entity/lore queries (locations, businesses, vehicles list, brands, sprunk) but is regularly outranked or matched by independent GTA6-only databases (gtabase.com, gta6bible.com, tracker.gg/gta6).
- **Extreme fan-site saturation**: at least ~55 distinct GTA6-dedicated domains surfaced across only 44 queries — this is one of the most fan-site-crowded gaming SERPs currently indexed, driven by the imminent Nov 19 2026 launch.
- **Map queries go to interactive map tools**: "gta 6 map" / "gta 6 locations" / "gta 6 interactive map" consistently surface dedicated map tools (gta6map.io, exploregta6.com, map.stateofleonida.net, gtavimap.com, gta6map.net/.xyz) ahead of or alongside big media.
- **Price/platform/release queries favor big media + a few price-specialist fan sites** (gta6post.com, gta6times.com) doing region-specific pricing (Canada) that general media skip.
- **Long-tail entity queries (vehicles, tattoo parlors, gang names) are almost entirely owned by dedicated databases/wikis** (tracker.gg/gta6, sixwiki.com, leonidawire.com, gta6bible.com, heistatlas.com) plus Fandom and social video (YouTube/TikTok/X/Instagram) for the music/culture long-tails (Se Me Nota, Devil Woman, ptt youngins).
- **Soundtrack/song queries are the one long-tail area gta6record.com should contest directly** — currently owned by redbull.com, gamespot.com, gtabase.com's radio-stations page, gtaboom.com's live tracker, and Spotify/YouTube; gta6record.com's soundtrack pages are indexed but did not surface in either "gta 6 soundtrack" or "gta 6 songs" top results.
- **Leaks/rumor tracking is its own sub-niche** with several single-purpose domains (cyberleek.us, gta6leaks.wiki, gta6game.net's leaks guide, gta6times.com's leaks page) — a content gap/opportunity area not yet covered by gta6record.com.
