## The short answer

**There is no honest ranking of the best cars in GTA 6, because Rockstar has
published no performance numbers at all.** No top speed. No acceleration. No
handling figure. Not for one vehicle.

What does exist is a short list of vehicles Rockstar has named itself, in its
own pre-order material and on its own site. That is what this page ranks: by
how solid the evidence is, not by a speed nobody has measured.

| Evidence level | Vehicles |
|---|---|
| Confirmed by Rockstar itself | 8 |
| Reported by major press from the Extended Look | 2 |
| Identified by the community from footage | 258 |
| Have a published top speed | 0 |

## Why most "best cars in GTA 6" lists are invented

An unreleased game has no measurable cars. To rank a car by speed you need to
drive it, and nobody outside Rockstar has driven any of these.

So the numbers on those pages come from one of three places:

1. **Copied from GTA 5.** A car with a similar name exists in the older game,
   and its stats are pasted across.
2. **Guessed from the shape.** The car looks fast in a trailer, so it is
   given a fast-sounding number.
3. **Taken from unofficial material.** This site does not use that, and it is
   not a measurement anyway.

None of those is a fact about GTA 6. A page that gives you a number to two
decimal places for a car nobody has driven is not being careful.

## If you want the best car, this is the real answer

The question people are really asking is "which car should I make sure I
get". That has a concrete answer, and it has nothing to do with speed.

**The only GTA 6 cars you can secure before launch are the ones inside a
pre-order edition or pack.** Everything else is earned or bought inside the
game after release, and nobody knows the prices yet.

- The **Ultimate Edition** carries the largest group: the Grotti Cheetah, the
  Dominator Buggy, the Ganado Retro Build, the Squalo, and Jason's Safehouse
  Vehicles.
- The **Vintage Vice City Pack** carries the '55 Vapid Stanier Sedan.

See the [Ultimate Edition page](/gta-6-ultimate-edition) for the full
contents and the [pre-order page](/gta-6-pre-order) for what each edition
costs and where to get it.

## The other 258 vehicles

The rest of the vehicle catalogue on this site was identified by the
community, frame by frame, from the trailers and official screenshots. That
work is genuine and it is worth recording, but it is a weaker standard than
Rockstar naming a car in print.

Each of those pages says so on its own face. You can browse the full set on
the [vehicles hub](/vehicles), and you can see where the footage they came
from was filmed on the [trailer locations map](/trailer-locations).

## What changes at launch

Once GTA 6 is out, three things become possible for the first time:

1. Real performance numbers, measured by players.
2. A complete vehicle list, rather than one built from footage.
3. Prices, unlock conditions, and where each car is found.

This page will be rewritten then. Until the game ships on 19 November 2026,
a speed ranking is a guess, and this site does not publish guesses as facts.
