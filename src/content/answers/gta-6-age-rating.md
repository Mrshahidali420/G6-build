## The short answer

**No age rating has been published for GTA 6.** No ESRB rating and no PEGI
rating exists yet, in any region. Anything you see claiming an official
rating right now is not real.

## What a rating actually is

An age rating is a label put on a game by an independent ratings board, not
by the game's own publisher. The board plays or reviews the game, or is shown
detailed content by the publisher, and then assigns a rating based on things
like violence, language, drug use, and sexual content. The rating tells
parents and retailers who the game is considered appropriate for. It is not
a review of quality, and it is not the same as an opinion piece calling a
game "mature" or "adult." It is a formal label with a defined process behind
it.

## Who rates games, region by region

Different regions use different boards, and a game usually needs a separate
rating in each one before it can be sold there.

| Region | Ratings body | What it looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| United States and Canada | ESRB | Letters such as E, T, M, or AO |
| Europe | PEGI | Age numbers such as PEGI 12, 16, or 18 |
| United Kingdom | PEGI, enforced by the BBFC in some cases | Same PEGI age numbers |
| Australia | Australian Classification Board | Ratings such as MA15+ or R18+ |
| India | No single mandatory ratings board for games | Publishers often display an age guide voluntarily, but there is no single national requirement the way there is for other regions |

None of these boards have published anything for GTA 6 as of this page's last
update. A rating from one region does not carry over to another. Each board
runs its own process, which is why a game can be rated in the United States
before it is rated in Europe, or the other way round.

## Why there is nothing to report yet

Ratings boards usually need a finished or near-finished build of a game
before they will assign a rating, along with detailed content descriptions
from the publisher. Rockstar has not confirmed how far along that process is
for GTA 6. Publishers also do not always announce ratings the moment they
are assigned. Sometimes a rating quietly appears on a retailer listing or a
ratings board's own database before any formal announcement is made.

That means a rating could, in principle, appear on an official database
without a big news story attached to it. This page tracks the official
ratings board listings directly, not just news coverage, and will be updated
the same day a real rating appears, with a link to the ratings board's own
listing as the source.

## What history suggests, clearly labelled as history

Every mainline Grand Theft Auto game released so far has carried an adult
rating in the regions that use one, meaning an ESRB rating of M for Mature,
and a PEGI rating of 18. That includes GTA IV and GTA V, both known for
intense violence, strong language, and adult themes.

That pattern is worth knowing, because it tells you what Rockstar's games
have looked like to ratings boards before. It is history, not a prediction.
This page will not state a rating for GTA 6 as if it were confirmed, because
it is not confirmed. A ratings board could, in theory, land somewhere
different this time, and nobody outside that board's own process can say for
certain until it happens.

## What to do if you see a rating claimed online

Treat any GTA 6 age rating you see right now as unconfirmed unless it links
directly to the ESRB's own site, PEGI's own site, or another official ratings
board database. A screenshot, a forum post, or a YouTube video is not a
source for a rating. If a real rating appears, it will show up on the
ratings board's own public database first, and that is the link this page
will use when it updates.

## The bottom line

As of this page's last update, GTA 6 has no published ESRB rating and no
published PEGI rating, anywhere. Every past mainline GTA game was rated for
adults, which is useful background, but it is not the same as a confirmed
rating for this one. This page will update the same day an official rating
is published, sourced directly from the ratings board.
