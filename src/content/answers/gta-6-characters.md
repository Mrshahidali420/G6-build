## The short answer

**Rockstar has named eleven people for Grand Theft Auto VI, on one page of
its own site.** That page is Only in Leonida, and it is the source for
everything below. Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos are the two playable leads.
Around them sits a small supporting cast tied to specific parts of Leonida:
Vice City and the Leonida Keys. This page lists only names that page
actually gives, and says plainly where the detail runs thin.

## Officially confirmed, by name

|---|---|---|
| Jason Duval | Playable lead. Former Army serviceman who ended up working for drug runners in the Keys | Leonida Keys |
| Lucia Caminos | Playable lead. Recently released from Leonida Penitentiary, fighting for a better life for her family | Not stated. Rockstar says her mother has wanted a better life since "their days in Liberty City" |

site, linked at the foot of this page. Nothing here comes from a leak, a
guess, or an older Grand Theft Auto game.

## The two leads

Jason and Lucia are the only two characters Rockstar has confirmed as
playable. Rockstar's bios say Jason grew up around grifters and crooks,
tried the Army to get away from a troubled adolescence, and ended up back in
familiar territory anyway, working for drug runners in the Keys. Lucia's
father taught her to fight, and that instinct got her into trouble
protecting her family, landing her in Leonida Penitentiary before she got
out. Both leads are described as wanting a way out of the life they are in,
and Rockstar's own framing puts their meeting at the center of the story.

## The Vice City side: Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan Priest, and Real Dimez

Boobie Ike is described as a local figure who turned his time in the streets
into a legitimate business spread across real estate, a strip club, and a
recording studio. His partner in the recording studio side is Dre'Quan
Priest, whom Rockstar describes as more of a hustler than a gangster, who
always wanted to break into music. Their label is called Only Raw Records,
and its first signed act is Real Dimez, a rap duo made up of Bae-Luxe and
Roxy. Rockstar's page says the two have been friends since high school and
built a following partly through an early hit single alongside a local
rapper named DWNPLY, who does not get a write-up of his own beyond that
mention.

## The Leonida Keys side: Brian Heder, Lori Heder, and Cal Hampton

Brian Heder is described as a longtime drug runner from an older era of
smuggling in the Keys, who still moves product through a boat yard and lets
Jason live rent free at one of his properties in exchange for help with
local shakedowns. His third wife, Lori Heder, is named inside his write-up
rather than given a page of her own. Cal Hampton is described as Jason's
friend and a fellow associate of Brian's, who prefers staying home,
listening to Coast Guard radio chatter, and keeping to himself.

## A separate name: Raul Bautista

Raul Bautista is described by Rockstar as an experienced, confident bank
robber who is always looking for people willing to take bigger risks for
bigger rewards. Unlike the other names above, Rockstar's page does not tie
him directly to Vice City or the Keys in the text of his bio.

  likely to grow.
