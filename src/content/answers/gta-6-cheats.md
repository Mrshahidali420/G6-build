## The short answer

**There are no GTA 6 cheat codes.** The game is not out. Nobody has a copy.
Nothing can be tested, so nothing can be true. Rockstar has published no
cheats for Grand Theft Auto VI, and it has never published cheats for any
game before that game shipped.

Every "GTA 6 cheat codes" list on the internet today was made up. Most of
them copy the button sequences from GTA 5 and change the title.

This page records that fact, records how cheats actually worked in the games
Rockstar has shipped, and will be updated with real codes once real codes
exist.

## Why a real list cannot exist yet

A cheat code is a sequence the game itself listens for. It is written into
the game's own code. To know one, you need the game.

That means only two groups can know a GTA 6 cheat before launch:

1. Rockstar, who wrote it.
2. Nobody else.

Rockstar has said nothing about cheats in GTA 6. No Newswire post, no entry
on the official site, no interview. Until the game is on sale, a third source
does not exist.

| Has Rockstar published GTA 6 cheats? | No |
| Has Rockstar said GTA 6 will have cheats? | No |
| Can a cheat list be tested by anybody? | No |
| Date the game is expected | 19 November 2026 |

## How cheats worked in the last GTA game

This is the useful part, because it is real. Grand Theft Auto V shipped with
two ways to use a cheat, and both are worth knowing if you plan to look for
GTA 6 cheats on day one.

### Two entry methods

**Button sequences.** On a controller, you pressed a fixed run of buttons
during play. The game showed a short confirmation. There was no menu and no
text box.

**In-game phone numbers.** On the character's phone, you dialled a number and
pressed call. The number acted as the code. This method was added in GTA V
and made cheats easier to share, because a number is easier to remember than
eleven button presses.

### The rules that came with them

| Rule in GTA 5 | What it meant |
|---|---|
| Cheats are not saved | Reload the game and every cheat is off again |
| Trophies and achievements lock | The session with a cheat in it earns nothing |
| Online is excluded | No single-player cheat ever worked in GTA Online |
| Some are one-way | A few could not be switched back off in the same session |

Rockstar has not said any of this carries into GTA 6. It is the record of the
last game, not a forecast of the next one.

## What kinds of cheats GTA games have had

Across the series, cheats have fallen into a small number of groups. Again,
this is history, not a GTA 6 list.

- **Player state.** Health, armour, invincibility for a set time.
- **Weapons.** A full weapon set given at once, or extra ammunition.
- **Wanted level.** Raise it or clear it.
- **Vehicles.** Spawn a named car, bike, helicopter or plane next to you.
- **World.** Weather, gravity, time of day, slow motion.
- **Fun.** Explosive rounds, drunk mode, and similar joke effects.

Notice what is missing from that list: money. No mainline Grand Theft Auto
game has ever had a working money cheat in single player. That is the exact
thing the scam sites promise.

## How to spot a fake GTA 6 cheat list

If you search this term before launch, you will find pages full of codes.
Here is how to read them.

- **It lists exact button sequences.** Nobody outside Rockstar can know one
  yet. A page that prints eleven buttons in order is printing an invention.
- **It offers money.** There is no money cheat, and there is no way to add
  money to a game that has not shipped.
- **It asks you to download something.** A cheat is typed into the game. It
  is never a file, an APK, or a program.
- **It asks for your account name.** No cheat has ever needed one. That is a
  login page wearing a costume.
- **It asks you to complete an offer or a survey.** That is the whole
  business model of the page. There is no reward at the end.
