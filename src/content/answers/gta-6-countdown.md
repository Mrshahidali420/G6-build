## The short answer

**Grand Theft Auto VI is due 19 November 2026**, and the counter above this
paragraph is running to it live. It counts to 00:00 UTC on that date, which is
the start of the calendar day Rockstar named, not a guess at an unlock hour in
any particular country. Rockstar has never published a time of day, so this
page does not print one.

On launch night, the [GTA 6 launch day hub](/gta-6-launch) answers "is GTA 6
out yet?" as each region unlocks.

## Why the counter targets UTC midnight, not a launch hour

Every other GTA 6 countdown you will find online has to make a choice: either
admit Rockstar has not said what hour the game unlocks, or quietly assume it
does and print a clock anyway. This page takes the first option.

Coordinated Universal Time, UTC, has no daylight saving and no regional bias,
so it is the only honest anchor for a date-only confirmation. The counter
above runs to the first second of 19 November 2026 in UTC. That is not the
moment anyone's copy of the game actually unlocks, and it is not the first
moment the date begins on Earth either: the timezones furthest east reach
19 November up to 14 hours before UTC does. It is simply one fixed, neutral
point that can be defended without inventing a detail Rockstar has not given.

## What the date means region by region

There is no confirmed unlock clock, so there is no honest hour-by-hour table
to print here. What can be said honestly is about the date itself, not the
time.

| Region | What 19 November 2026 means |
| --- | --- |
| North America, Europe, most of Africa, most of Asia | The confirmed release day, 19 November 2026, falls on the same calendar date locally as it does in UTC, for all or nearly all of that day. |
| Australia, New Zealand, and other timezones ahead of UTC | 19 November 2026 begins locally several hours before it begins in UTC, so the date itself still lands on 19 November where you are. |
| The far western edge of the map: Hawaii, parts of the US Pacific timezone, and similar timezones well behind UTC | If Rockstar follows the midnight-local unlock pattern used by some past Rockstar launches, content could become available late on 18 November local time rather than on 19 November. Nothing has confirmed that pattern will repeat for this game. |

Read that last row carefully. It is a description of what a past pattern would
imply if it repeats, not a confirmed detail about this launch. The GTA 6
release date page covers the same open question about unlock timing in more
depth.

## The date's history, in brief

GTA 6's launch date has moved twice, and both moves were announced directly by
Rockstar or its parent company Take-Two, not leaked.

| Target | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Autumn 2025 | The original window Rockstar gave. Never hit. |
| 26 May 2026 | The first replacement date, announced after the autumn 2025 window passed. Also moved. |
| 19 November 2026 | The current date, announced around 6 November 2025 and repeated when pre-orders opened on 25 June 2026. |

Take-Two chief executive Strauss Zelnick has said publicly that the company
does not expect another delay. That is a statement of intent from the
publisher, not a guarantee. The full delay history, with sourcing for each
date, is on the GTA 6 delayed page.

## What is still not known

- **No unlock hour**, for any region. This page's counter deliberately targets
  UTC midnight rather than filling that gap with a guess.
- **No confirmation that a past midnight-local unlock pattern will repeat**
  for this launch, in any region.
- **No third date change** has been announced since the move to 19 November
  2026, but nothing rules one out.

## How this page stays honest

The counter targets the one thing Rockstar has actually confirmed, the
calendar date, and states plainly that no unlock hour exists to count down to
instead. The static version of this page, the one a crawler or a reader with
JavaScript off receives, shows the correct day count at the moment the page
was built, so the number is never blank or wrong for anyone. Every date claim
above traces to a Rockstar Newswire post, a Bloomberg report, or a direct
Take-Two statement, linked at the foot of this page. If the date changes, the
counter's target changes the same day.
