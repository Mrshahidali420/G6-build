## The short answer

**Rockstar has not announced whether GTA 6 will support crossplay between
PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.** The two platforms are confirmed for the
game itself, launching 19 November 2026, but nothing has been said about
whether players on one can play online with players on the other.

What follows is what is actually confirmed, plus some honest background on
how GTA Online, the previous game in the series, has handled players moving
between consoles. That background is about GTA Online, not GTA 6, and the two
should not be mixed up.

## Press reported

No outlet has reported a Rockstar crossplay announcement for GTA 6, because
none has been made. GTA Wiki's Grand Theft Auto VI page, one of the most
detailed running trackers of confirmed GTA 6 details, has no section on
crossplay or cross-platform play.

What press coverage does exist concerns GTA Online, not GTA 6, and it points
the other way from what many players assume:

## Background: how GTA Online handled PlayStation and Xbox

This section is about Grand Theft Auto Online, the online mode of a previous
game, not about GTA 6. It is included because it is the only sourced,
relevant history available, and because it directly answers what a lot of
people are really asking: has Rockstar ever let PlayStation and Xbox players
share an online space in a GTA game.

GTA Online released across PlayStation 3, PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Xbox
360, Xbox One, and Xbox Series X|S, among other platforms, according to GTA
Wiki's platform listing for the game. When PS5 and Xbox Series X|S launched,
Rockstar let players migrate a GTA Online character's progress from the
previous console generation to the new one, PS4 to PS5, or Xbox One to Xbox
Series X|S. GamesRadar+ reported plainly that this migration process did not
allow for cross-progression or crossplay between the console families. Money
spent on in-game currency only carried over when the migration stayed within
the same family, PlayStation to PlayStation, or Xbox to Xbox.

In other words, the record here shows same-brand progress transfer, not
cross-brand play. Nothing in the sources checked for this page shows
PlayStation and Xbox players sharing a live GTA Online session together.

## Why this does not answer the GTA 6 question

A pattern from a previous game's online mode is context, not a promise about
GTA 6. Rockstar has not said GTA 6 will repeat GTA Online's approach, and it
has not said it will do anything differently either. Console makers, not just
Rockstar, are also part of whether crossplay happens, since a crossplay
feature needs agreement from both PlayStation and Xbox's online services as
well as the developer. None of that has been discussed publicly for GTA 6.

## What is still not known

- Whether GTA 6 will support crossplay between PlayStation 5 and Xbox
  Series X|S, in single-player, multiplayer, or both
- Whether GTA 6's online component, if PlayStation and Xbox players end up
  sharing anything, will work the way GTA Online's console migration worked
- Whether PS5 Pro is treated any differently from a standard PS5 for any
  online or crossplay purposes
