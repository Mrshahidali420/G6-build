## The short answer

**GTA 6 is not out yet.** It launches on **19 November 2026** on PS5 and
Xbox Series X|S. Preload opens on 12 November. On PS5 it unlocks at midnight
local time, region by region, starting in New Zealand.

This page is the launch week in one place. The box at the top tells you if
the game is out. The table below lists every dated step, each with a source.
The log further down gets new lines on launch day.

## What is not announced yet

### The launch trailer

Rockstar has not announced a launch trailer for GTA 6. The newest official
video is An Extended Look, from 27 August 2026. All of them are on our
[GTA 6 trailers](/gta-6-trailers) page.

This is the past pattern, and only a pattern. For GTA 5, a video called The
Official Trailer came out on 29 August 2013, 19 days before the 17 September
launch. For Red Dead Redemption 2, the launch trailer came out on 18 October
2018, 8 days before the 26 October launch. Nothing says GTA 6 will follow
either one.

### The review embargo

No review date has been announced. Past pattern again: GTA 5 reviews went up
on 16 September 2013, and Red Dead Redemption 2 reviews on 25 October 2018.
Both landed one day before launch. It is a pattern, not a promise.

### The day one patch

Rockstar has not said if there will be a day one patch. No patch notes and no
patch size are out. Plan for one more download on launch night, just in case,
but treat that as a guess until Rockstar says more.

### Midnight launch in shops

No shop midnight opening has been confirmed by a source we checked. It
matters less this time. The boxed copy is a download code, so you can buy it
from 12 November and preload at home. A midnight queue gets you nothing a
code bought a week early does not.

## Physical copies arrive a week early

Take-Two says the boxed copy goes on sale from 12 November 2026, the same day
preload opens. There is no full game disc in the box, only a download code.
So a physical copy still needs the full download. Buy it early, redeem the
code, and let it preload. It still unlocks on launch day, at the same time
as a digital copy in your region.

## How the "Is GTA 6 out yet?" box works

The box at the top has three answers. Before 11:00 UTC on 18 November it
says not yet. From then until 06:00 UTC on 19 November it says the game is
unlocking region by region, because it is live in New Zealand but not yet in
the US. After that it says yes. It checks your own clock, so it changes on
time even if this page has not been rebuilt.
