## The short answer

**Rockstar has not published a square kilometre figure for the GTA 6 map.**
No official size number exists. What is confirmed is the setting: the game
takes place in the fictional state of Leonida, with Vice City as its main
city, alongside several other named destinations.

## Why there is no size figure

Rockstar's pattern with past games is to reveal a world through trailers,
screenshots and hands-on previews, not through a stated area figure. That has
not changed for GTA 6. The official Rockstar site and the Only in Leonida page
describe the setting and its locations in detail, but neither states an area
in square kilometres or square miles.

Any number circulating online for "the GTA 6 map size" is either a guess, a
comparison pulled from an unrelated leak, or a calculation someone made from
unofficial file analysis. None of that carries Rockstar's confirmation, so
none of it belongs on this page as fact.

## What Rockstar has actually confirmed

This is the full list of locations Rockstar has officially named for GTA 6,
each backed by dedicated official screenshots, artwork, or site copy.

| Place | What it is | Confirmed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leonida | The fictional state that the whole game is set in | Rockstar's official GTA VI site |
| Vice City | The main city, and the best-known location in the game | Rockstar's official GTA VI site |
| Leonida Keys | A tropical island chain within Leonida | Official screenshot set and artwork |
| Port Gellhorn | A worn-down Gulf Coast area with roadside tourism | Official screenshot set and artwork |
| Grassrivers | A wetland region outside the main city | Official screenshot set and artwork |
| Ambrosia | An inland, industrial area | Official screenshot set and artwork |
| Mount Kalaga National Park | A northern wilderness and national park area | Official screenshot set and artwork |

That is seven named places in total: one state, one main city inside it, and
five further destinations. Rockstar has not published a map showing how these
places connect, their exact borders, or their distances from one another.

## How Rockstar has revealed map details in the past

Rockstar has not published a size figure for GTA 6, but it helps to know how
the studio has handled this in earlier games. For GTA 5, Rockstar did not
release an official square kilometre figure either. Fan communities measured
the in-game map themselves after release, using in-game tools and distance
markers, and those community figures became the widely quoted numbers many
people now treat as fact. Rockstar itself never issued its own square
kilometre statement for that game.

That pattern matters here because it shows the most likely path for GTA 6 too.
A precise area figure, if one ever becomes common knowledge, will probably
come from players measuring the finished map after release, not from a
Rockstar press release before launch. Anyone promising a confirmed GTA 6 map
size months before release is getting ahead of what Rockstar actually does.

## Why unofficial size estimates should be treated carefully

Searches for "GTA 6 map size" often turn up confident-looking numbers, usually
framed as square miles or square kilometres and sometimes compared directly
against GTA 5. It is worth knowing where these numbers tend to come from
before trusting one.

- **Leak-based estimates.** Some numbers trace back to unverified file leaks
  from years ago, long before Rockstar's official reveals. These predate
  confirmed locations like Leonida Keys, Port Gellhorn, Grassrivers, Ambrosia
  and Mount Kalaga National Park, so they cannot reflect the final game.
- **Comparison guesses.** Other numbers are simply someone comparing trailer
  footage to GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 and estimating a multiplier, with
  no measurement behind it at all.
- **Confident phrasing does not mean confirmed.** A number written in a
  headline or a video title is not automatically a Rockstar fact. Always check
  whether a claim traces back to Rockstar, Sony or Microsoft directly.

This page only states what carries a real, named, official source. That is
why it lists seven confirmed places instead of one invented number.

## Leonida, the state

Leonida is the setting for the entire game. Rockstar's official site describes
the story as taking place across the state, with Vice City sitting inside it.
Leonida is loosely inspired by Florida, in the same way GTA 5's Los Santos was
inspired by Los Angeles and GTA: Vice City's original setting was inspired by
Miami. Rockstar has not released a finished, edge-to-edge map of Leonida, so
there is no way to state its size honestly.

## Vice City, the main city

Vice City is the central urban setting and the location most closely tied to
protagonists Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. Rockstar has released a dedicated
screenshot set and an official postcard for Vice City, and it is the location
most featured in trailer footage so far. No district list, city boundary, or
road map has been published for it.

## The five other confirmed destinations

Beyond Vice City, Rockstar has named five further places inside Leonida.

- **Leonida Keys.** A tropical island region with boats, bars, beaches and
  wildlife, tied in official material to protagonist Jason's criminal work.
- **Port Gellhorn.** A rundown Gulf Coast area with cheap tourism and roadside
  businesses.
- **Grassrivers.** A wetland region, visually distinct from the dense streets
  of Vice City.
- **Ambrosia.** An inland, industrial area with a working-class feel.
- **Mount Kalaga National Park.** A wilderness area in the north of the state,
  shown across six official screenshots.

Each of these has its own dedicated official screenshot set and artwork
postcard from Rockstar, which is why they count as confirmed rather than
guessed. What Rockstar has not shown is exact boundaries, road networks, or
how large any one of them is relative to the others.

## What is still not known

- No square kilometre or square mile figure for the total map.
- No official map graphic showing how Leonida, Vice City and the other five
  destinations connect to each other.
- No confirmation of whether more destinations exist beyond the seven named
  places above.
- No comparison figure against GTA 5's map or any other Rockstar game.
- No confirmed travel times or distances between locations.

## Why this matters more than it seems

A lot of GTA 6 map size claims online are dressed up to look precise, often
with a specific square kilometre number attached to make them sound
researched. None of those numbers trace back to Rockstar. Sticking to the
seven officially named places, and being upfront that no area figure exists,
is the only honest way to answer this question right now. This page will be
updated the moment Rockstar, Sony or Microsoft publishes anything closer to a
real map size.
