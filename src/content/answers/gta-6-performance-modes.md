## The short answer

**Rockstar has not published an official frame rate or performance mode for
Grand Theft Auto VI.** What exists instead is a press report: Rockstar North
co-director Rob Nelson reportedly told journalists after Rockstar's August
2026 preview showings that GTA 6 is currently targeting 30 frames per second
on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and that he could not yet commit to
whether a separate, higher frame rate performance mode would be available at
launch.

That is a comment reported by press, not a specification Rockstar has
published on its own site. The two are marked separately below, because
blurring them is exactly what makes this question hard to answer honestly
elsewhere.

## What "performance mode" and "fidelity mode" mean

These two terms come up a lot in questions about GTA 6, so it is worth
defining them plainly. On PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, some games let players
choose between two display options: one that favours a higher, steadier frame
rate at the cost of some visual detail, usually called a performance mode,
and one that favours higher resolution or visual detail at a lower frame
rate, usually called a fidelity mode. Whether GTA 6 will offer a choice like
this is exactly the part Nelson reportedly said was undecided.

## What is still not known

- Whether the reported 30fps target is final, or changes before launch
- Whether a 60fps performance mode will exist at launch, be added later, or
  never ship
- The confirmed resolution GTA 6 runs at, on any platform or mode
- Whether PS5 Pro gets any different treatment from a standard PS5
- Whether Rockstar will ever publish an official frame rate or resolution
  figure on its own site, rather than leaving it to reported comments
