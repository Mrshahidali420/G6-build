## The short answer

**GTA 6 pre-orders have been open since 25 June 2026.** Every pre-order gets a
free bonus pack, and prices depend on the edition and your country.

You can pre-order now through the PlayStation Store, the Xbox Store, or a
participating retailer. The game itself releases on 19 November 2026.

## When pre-orders opened

Rockstar Games opened pre-orders on 25 June 2026, worldwide, for both
PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The date and the prices were announced
together on Rockstar's own Newswire the day before, on 24 June 2026. That is a
direct, official announcement, not a leak or a retailer guess.

## The two editions and what they cost

There are two editions. Prices vary by country because of tax and currency
rules, but the pattern is the same everywhere: the Ultimate Edition costs about
20 to 25 percent more than the Standard Edition.

| Region | Standard Edition | Ultimate Edition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| United States | 79.99 USD | 99.99 USD |
| United Kingdom | 69.99 GBP | 89.99 GBP |
| Europe | 79.99 EUR | 99.99 EUR |
| Canada | 109.99 CAD | 139.99 CAD |
| Australia | 129.95 AUD | 159.95 AUD |
| India | Rs 5,999 | Rs 7,499 |

The US, UK, and Europe prices come straight from Rockstar's own Newswire and
store listings. The Canada, Australia, and India prices are reported by gaming
press rather than confirmed on Rockstar's own site, so treat those as reliable
but not official.

## What the Ultimate Edition adds

The Ultimate Edition is not a separate game. It is the same game with extra
content bundled in. According to the PlayStation Store listing, it adds
exclusive vehicles, weapons, apparel, and businesses, plus two extra side
missions that are not part of the Standard Edition.

If you only care about the main story and do not want extra cosmetic or side
content, the Standard Edition gets you the same core game for less money.

## What every pre-order includes

This is the part that applies no matter which edition or platform you choose.
Anyone who pre-orders before 20 November 2026 gets the **Vintage Vice City
Pack** for free. Rockstar's own support page lists what is in it:

- A 1955 Vapid Stanier sedan, plus a garage to store it in
- Access to Ocean Beach, an area of the map
- Outfits and hairstyles for both Jason and Lucia, the game's two lead
  characters
- A themed weapon pattern

This pack is not sold separately. The only way to get it is to pre-order
before the cut-off date. Once 20 November 2026 passes, which is the day after
launch, it is gone.

## The GTA+ bonus, and why it only applies to digital

On top of the Vintage Vice City Pack, anyone who pre-orders **digitally** also
gets one free month of GTA+, Rockstar's subscription service. This bonus is
tied to digital pre-orders specifically. Rockstar's Newswire lists it as part
of the digital pre-order offer, and it is not mentioned as part of the
physical pre-order bonus.

If getting the free GTA+ month matters to you, pre-order digitally rather than
picking up a physical copy at a shop.

## Digital versus physical: the part people miss

A lot of buyers assume a physical pre-order means a disc with the whole game
on it, ready to play the moment they get home. That is not how GTA 6 physical
copies work.

According to GameSpot's reporting, physical copies of GTA 6 ship with a
**download code**, not the full game on disc. You still need an internet
connection and enough free storage to download the game after you put the
disc in, or redeem the code. The disc itself is not a complete offline copy.

This matters for planning. If you are buying physical because you have a slow
or limited internet connection, a physical pre-order will not solve that
problem. You will still be downloading the bulk of the game on release day, the
same as anyone who bought digitally.

## Where to pre-order

Pre-orders are live through the official digital storefronts and through
retailers that stock the physical edition:

- PlayStation Store, for PS5
- Xbox Store, for Xbox Series X|S
- Physical retailers, where the game is sold as either edition on disc

The prices listed above are the official prices for digital purchases through
PlayStation and Xbox. Retailer pricing for physical copies can vary and is not
something Rockstar controls or publishes centrally.

## What is not known about cancelling or getting a refund

Rockstar has not published a GTA 6 specific policy on cancelling a pre-order or
getting a refund. Nothing in Rockstar's own announcements covers this.

In practice, this means a pre-order almost certainly falls under the normal
refund rules of wherever you bought it. PlayStation Store, Xbox Store, and
individual retailers each have their own standard refund policies for
pre-orders, and those would apply here the same as they would to any other
game. But that is an assumption based on how digital storefronts normally
work, not a confirmed GTA 6 specific answer. If you are relying on being able
to cancel, check the refund terms of the exact store you are buying from
before you pay.

## Standard or Ultimate: which one to pick

If money is tight, the Standard Edition gets you the full game, the same
story, the same map, and the same two lead characters. You are not missing any
part of the main game by skipping Ultimate.

The Ultimate Edition makes more sense if you know you enjoy collecting
cosmetic extras and extra vehicles from day one, or if the two bonus side
missions matter to you. Neither edition changes the release date. Both unlock
on 19 November 2026, and both come with the Vintage Vice City Pack if you
pre-order before 20 November 2026.

## What is still unconfirmed

A few things about pre-ordering are simply not public yet:

- Whether there will be any additional pre-order bonuses announced closer to
  launch, beyond the Vintage Vice City Pack
- Exact retailer-by-retailer physical stock or shipping dates
- Any regional pricing outside the countries listed above

If Rockstar changes any pre-order term, this page will be updated the same
day, and the checked date at the top will move with it.
