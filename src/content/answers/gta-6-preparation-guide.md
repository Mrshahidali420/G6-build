## The short answer

There are five things worth doing before GTA 6 launches, and only one of them
is urgent right now.

1. **Free up storage.** This is the one that bites people.
2. **Check your console is one of the two that will run it.**
3. **Decide whether you want a pre-order edition, or not.**
4. **Know when preload opens, so the download is not the thing that stops you.**
5. **Set a budget, because the edition prices differ.**

The date to work back from is **19 November 2026**. Each step below links to
the page on this site that carries the detail and the source.

## 1. Storage is the real job

Rockstar has not published a file size for GTA 6. That is a fact, not an
oversight on this page, and anybody quoting an exact number in gigabytes
right now is guessing.

What is safe to say: plan for a large install. Grand Theft Auto V grew well
past 100 GB on current consoles over its life. A newer, larger game is not
going to be smaller.

**Do this now, not on launch day.** Deleting games takes minutes, but
re-downloading the ones you still want takes hours, and everybody's internet
is busy on a launch day.

- [How big is GTA 6 going to be](/gta-6-file-size) covers what is known and
  what is not.
- [Best PS5 SSD for GTA 6](/ps5-ssd-for-gta-6) covers adding storage rather
  than deleting things, if that suits you better.

## 3. Decide about pre-ordering

A pre-order is optional. It buys you two things and nothing else:

- **Extra content.** The Ultimate Edition and the Vintage Vice City Pack each
  contain vehicles, a garage, outfits and other items.
- **A preload slot.** A pre-ordered digital copy can usually download before
  launch day.

It does not get you the game early, and it does not get you a discount.

- [GTA 6 pre-order](/gta-6-pre-order) for what each edition holds.
- [GTA 6 Ultimate Edition](/gta-6-ultimate-edition) for the largest bundle.
- [Best cars in GTA 6](/best-cars-in-gta-6) for which vehicles are tied to
  which pack.

## 4. Know the preload window

Preload is the download that happens before the game unlocks. It is the
difference between playing at midnight and waiting until the next morning.

Preload only applies to digital copies. It usually opens a few days before
release, and it needs the storage from step one to already be free.

- [GTA 6 preload](/gta-6-preload) for how it works and what is known about
  timing.
- [GTA 6 countdown](/gta-6-countdown) for the live count to the date.

## What is not on this list, and why

You will see preparation guides that tell you to replay GTA 5, read the
story so far, or study the map. None of that is preparation. It is something
to do while waiting, which is fine, but it does not change whether you can
play on launch day.

You will also see guides that quote an exact install size, an exact preload
time, or an exact server-unlock hour. Rockstar has published none of those.
Where this site does not know a number, it says so.

If you want the confirmed facts rather than a checklist, use the
[confirmed list](/confirmed), which holds only what Rockstar said itself, and
the [release date page](/gta-6-release-date) for the date and its history.
