## The short answer

**GTA 6 costs A$129.95 for the Standard Edition and A$159.95 for the Ultimate
Edition in Australia.** Those are the Australian PlayStation Store prices,
and the Xbox Store matched them when pre-orders opened. The game releases on
19 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## Australia price table

| Edition | Price in AUD | What is in it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Edition | A$129.95 | The full game, plus the Vintage Vice City Pack and one month of GTA+ with a pre-order |
| Ultimate Edition | A$159.95 | Everything in Standard, plus the Ultimate Edition Upgrade |
| Ultimate Edition Upgrade (bought later) | Not announced | Sold separately to Standard owners, price not set |

The gap between the two editions is A$30. In the US it is 20 USD.

## Where the Australian price comes from

The Australian PlayStation Store listing shows A$129.95 and A$159.95 next to
each edition. GadgetGuy, Press Start and Stevivor all reported the same
numbers on 25 June 2026, the day pre-orders opened.

## Where to buy GTA 6 in Australia

When pre-orders went live, GadgetGuy checked the main Australian listings.

| Store | Standard Edition | Ultimate Edition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PlayStation Store | A$129.95 | A$159.95 |
| Xbox Store | A$129.95 | A$159.95 |
| EB Games | A$129.95 | Not listed in the report |
| JB Hi-Fi | A$129.95 | Not listed in the report |
| The Gamesmen | A$129.95 | Not listed in the report |
| Big W | A$125 | Not listed in the report |

These are prices from launch day of pre-orders. Retailers change their
prices, so check before you pay. The physical box holds a download code, not
a disc, so you still download the game.

## New Zealand prices

Stevivor also reported New Zealand prices: NZ$139.95 for the Standard Edition
and NZ$174.95 for the Ultimate Edition. New Zealand is also the first place
in the world where GTA 6 unlocks on PS5.

## What the Ultimate Edition adds

The extra A$30 buys single-player items for Jason and Lucia. Press Start
listed them: the '95 Grotti Cheetah, the '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy, the
Shitzu Squalo boat, the Rideout Customs and One-Eyed Willie's mod shops, the
Stock 305 clothing store, Electric Fang Tattoo, Sara's Unisex Salon and more.
The full list, and who it suits, is on our
[GTA 6 Ultimate Edition page](/gta-6-ultimate-edition).

## Pre-order extras in Australia

A pre-order of either edition placed before 20 November 2026 gets the free
Vintage Vice City Pack. A digital pre-order also adds one month of GTA+.

## What is not confirmed yet

- The Australian price of the Ultimate Edition Upgrade on its own.
- The Xbox unlock time. Only PlayStation has shown one. See our
  [GTA 6 release time page](/gta-6-release-time).
- Any launch sale. None has been announced.
