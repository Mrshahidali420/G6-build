## The short answer

**A GTA 6 pre-order costs CA$109.99 for the Standard Edition and CA$139.99
for the Ultimate Edition in Canada.** Those are the Canadian PlayStation
Store prices, before tax. The game releases on 19 November 2026 on PS5 and
Xbox Series X|S.

## Canada price table

| Edition | Price in CAD | What is in it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Edition | CA$109.99 | The full game, plus the Vintage Vice City Pack and one month of GTA+ with a pre-order |
| Ultimate Edition | CA$139.99 | Everything in Standard, plus the Ultimate Edition Upgrade |
| Ultimate Edition Upgrade (bought later) | Not announced | Sold separately to Standard owners, price not set |

The gap between the two editions is CA$30. In the US it is 20 USD.

## Where the Canadian price comes from

The Canadian PlayStation Store listing shows CA$109.99 and CA$139.99 next to
each edition. MobileSyrup reported the same numbers the day pre-orders
opened, on 25 June 2026, and Stevivor listed the same Standard price.

## Add tax at checkout

Canadian store prices are shown before sales tax, the same way US prices
are. What you actually pay depends on your province. So your final total
will be higher than CA$109.99 or CA$139.99.

## Where to buy GTA 6 in Canada

- **PlayStation Store Canada** for the PS5 digital version.
- **Xbox Store Canada** for the Xbox Series X|S digital version.
- **Retailers** for the physical Standard Edition.

The physical box holds a download code, not a disc. Retailers set their own
prices on physical stock, so a shop price may differ from the store price.

## What the Ultimate Edition adds

The CA$30 extra buys single-player items for Jason and Lucia. The list
includes the '95 Grotti Cheetah, the '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy, the Shitzu
Squalo boat, two exclusive vehicle mod shops, a clothing store, a tattoo
shop and a salon. The full list, and who it suits, is on our
[GTA 6 Ultimate Edition page](/gta-6-ultimate-edition).

## Pre-order extras in Canada

A pre-order of either edition placed before 20 November 2026 gets the free
Vintage Vice City Pack. A digital pre-order also adds one month of GTA+.

## What is not confirmed yet

- The Canadian price of the Ultimate Edition Upgrade on its own.
- Any launch discount. None has been announced.
- The Xbox unlock time. Only PlayStation has shown a timestamp so far. See
  our [GTA 6 release time page](/gta-6-release-time).
