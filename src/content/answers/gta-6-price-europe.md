## The short answer

**GTA 6 costs €79.99 for the Standard Edition and €99.99 for the Ultimate
Edition in euro countries.** Those prices are on the PlayStation Store in
Germany and Ireland, with VAT included. The game releases on 19 November 2026
on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## Euro price table

| Edition | Price in EUR | What is in it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Edition | €79.99 | The full game, plus the Vintage Vice City Pack and one month of GTA+ with a pre-order |
| Ultimate Edition | €99.99 | Everything in Standard, plus the Ultimate Edition Upgrade |
| Ultimate Edition Upgrade (bought later) | Not announced | Sold separately to Standard owners, price not set |

The number is the same as the US price, 79.99, but in euros and with VAT
already added. So it costs more in real money than the US price.

## Where the euro price comes from

We checked two euro PlayStation Store listings, Germany and Ireland. Both
show €79.99 and €99.99. Beebom's global price list reports the same euro
prices across the EU region. We did not check every euro country one by one,
so if your country shows a different number, your store is right and this
page is wrong.

## Where to buy GTA 6 in Europe

- **PlayStation Store** for the PS5 digital version.
- **Xbox Store** for the Xbox Series X|S digital version.
- **Retailers** for the physical Standard Edition.

The physical box holds a download code, not a disc. Shops set their own
prices on physical stock, so a shop price may differ from the store price.

## What the Ultimate Edition adds

The extra €20 buys single-player items for Jason and Lucia. That includes the
'95 Grotti Cheetah, the '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy, the Shitzu Squalo boat,
two exclusive vehicle mod shops, an exclusive clothing store, a tattoo shop
and a salon. The full list, and who it suits, is on our
[GTA 6 Ultimate Edition page](/gta-6-ultimate-edition).

## Pre-order extras in Europe

A pre-order of either edition placed before 20 November 2026 gets the free
Vintage Vice City Pack. A digital pre-order also adds one month of GTA+.

## When it unlocks in Europe

Going by the PlayStation Store, most of mainland Europe unlocks at midnight
CET on 19 November 2026. Greece, Finland, Romania, Bulgaria and Cyprus
unlock an hour earlier, at their own midnight. Full times are on our
[GTA 6 release time page](/gta-6-release-time).

## What is not confirmed yet

- The price of the Ultimate Edition Upgrade on its own.
- Official store prices for every non-euro country. The table above is press
  reported.
- Any launch sale. None has been announced.
