## The short answer

**Indian gaming outlets report GTA 6 at Rs 5,999 for the Standard Edition and
Rs 7,499 for the Ultimate Edition.** Rockstar itself has not published these
figures. Treat them as likely, not locked in, until an official Rockstar page
confirms them.

## Why this page is careful with the word "official"

Some GTA 6 prices are as solid as facts get before launch. The US price of
79.99 USD and the UK price of GBP 69.99 both trace back to Rockstar's own
Newswire post or Rockstar's own storefronts. The India price does not have
that paper trail yet.

91mobiles, a well known Indian tech and gaming outlet, reported Rs 5,999 and
Rs 7,499 as the pre-order figures showing up on Indian storefronts. That is a
real report from a real outlet, and it is worth taking seriously. It is just
not the same as Rockstar stating the number itself. This page will keep saying
that plainly instead of dressing the number up as confirmed.

## The two India prices

| Edition | Reported price | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Edition | Rs 5,999 | Medium, press reported only |
| Ultimate Edition | Rs 7,499 | Medium, press reported only |

Both figures cover the same content difference as everywhere else. The
Ultimate Edition is reported to add exclusive vehicles, weapons, apparel and
businesses, plus two extra side missions, matching what Rockstar has confirmed
for the edition globally on its PlayStation Store listing.

## How Rs 5,999 compares to the US price

It is tempting to look at Rs 5,999 next to 79.99 USD and assume India is
getting a steep discount, because the rupee number looks small on its own.
That is not really true once you convert it properly.

Rs 5,999 converts to a figure much closer to the US price than a "discount"
would suggest, once you run the maths at a normal exchange rate. It looks
smaller only because the rupee is a smaller unit of currency than the dollar.
This is the same regional pricing pattern seen in other countries on our full
GTA 6 price page: publishers set a number that looks reasonable in the local
currency, not a straight currency conversion of the US price, and not
necessarily a discount either.

So while Rs 5,999 is a lower number than Rs 6,650 would be at a flat
conversion, it is not obviously a bargain compared to what a player in the US
pays. It sits in roughly the same range once you account for the exchange
rate.

## Where to pre-order in India

Two storefronts are confirmed to be carrying GTA 6 listings that Indian
players can pre-order through.

- **PlayStation Store India.** The PS5 version can be pre-ordered directly
  through the Indian PlayStation Store.
- **Xbox Store India.** The Xbox Series X|S version can be pre-ordered
  through the Indian Xbox Store in the same way.

Physical copies are expected to be available through normal retail channels
in India, the way past Rockstar games have been, since Rockstar has confirmed
physical copies exist worldwide and ship with a download code rather than a
full disc. We do not have a sourced price for a specific physical retailer in
India, so this page will not invent one. If you are shopping at a specific
store, check that store's own listing for its price rather than assuming it
matches the press-reported Rs 5,999 figure.

## What is not known about the India release

- No Rockstar Newswire post has named an India price directly.
- No confirmed age rating exists for India or anywhere else yet.
- No confirmed unlock time exists for any region, including India.
- No PC version, PC price, or PC date has been announced anywhere, so there is
  nothing to convert for a PC release in India either.

## Digital versus physical in India

The Rs 5,999 and Rs 7,499 figures reported for India apply to the digital
storefront listings on PlayStation Store India and Xbox Store India.
Rockstar has confirmed, for the release generally, that physical copies exist
and ship with a download code rather than a full disc, but no India specific
physical price has been reported by any outlet we can point to. If you are
planning to buy a physical copy in India, check the retailer's own listing
closer to launch rather than assuming it will match the digital storefront
number.

## Why India often gets a separate regional price at all

Publishers set India specific prices for the same reason they set separate
UK or EU prices: local purchasing power, local tax rules, and what the
regional market is used to paying all factor in. India frequently sees lower
absolute prices than the US or UK for big releases, not because the game is
worth less there, but because a flat US price converted directly would be out
of reach for a much larger share of Indian players. This is a standard
practice across the games industry, not something specific to Rockstar or to
GTA 6.

That said, standard practice is not the same as a confirmed number. The
pattern gives you a reason to believe Rs 5,999 is plausible. It does not make
it official.

## Currency risk before launch

Rs 5,999 was reported at a specific point in time. Exchange rates move, and
digital storefronts do not always update local prices the moment a currency
shifts. Between now and 19 November 2026, it is possible a storefront price
in India could be adjusted up or down slightly, especially since Rockstar has
not locked the number in with an official post the way it has for the US and
UK. If you are budgeting closely, check the storefront listing again close to
launch rather than relying only on the figure reported months earlier.

## What to do with this information

If you are planning a pre-order in India, Rs 5,999 and Rs 7,499 are the best
numbers currently available, and they came from a specific, named outlet
rather than a rumour with no source. Just do not treat them as guaranteed
until Rockstar itself publishes an India price, the way it has already done
for the United States and the United Kingdom. This page will update the
moment that happens.
