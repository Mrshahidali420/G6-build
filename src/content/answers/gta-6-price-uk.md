## The short answer

**GTA 6 costs £69.99 for the Standard Edition and £89.99 for the Ultimate
Edition in the UK.** Both prices are live on the UK PlayStation Store. The
game releases on 19 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## UK price table

| Edition | UK price | What is in it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard Edition | £69.99 | The full game, plus the Vintage Vice City Pack and one month of GTA+ with a pre-order |
| Ultimate Edition | £89.99 | Everything in Standard, plus the Ultimate Edition Upgrade |
| Ultimate Edition Upgrade (bought later) | Not announced | Sold separately to Standard owners, price not set |

The gap between the two editions is £20. That is the same gap as in the US,
where the prices are 79.99 USD and 99.99 USD.

## Where the UK price comes from

This is a firm number. The UK PlayStation Store listing shows £69.99 and
£89.99 next to each edition. TechRadar reported the same prices on the day
UK pre-orders opened. Rockstar's own Newswire post gave the US price, and the
UK store listing is the official UK equivalent.

## Where to buy GTA 6 in the UK

- **PlayStation Store** for the PS5 digital version.
- **Xbox Store** for the Xbox Series X|S digital version.
- **High street and online retailers** for the physical Standard Edition.

The physical box holds a download code, not a disc. You still download the
whole game. Retailers can set their own prices on physical stock, so a shop
price may be a little higher or lower than the store price above.

## Does the UK price include VAT?

Yes. UK store prices always include VAT. That is one reason £69.99 does not
match a straight currency conversion of 79.99 USD. The US figure is before
sales tax, so the two numbers are not measured the same way.

## What the extra £20 buys

The Ultimate Edition adds a long list of single-player extras for Jason and
Lucia. That includes the '95 Grotti Cheetah, the '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy
with its own garage, the Shitzu Squalo boat, two exclusive vehicle mod shops,
an exclusive clothing store, a tattoo shop and a salon. Our
[GTA 6 Ultimate Edition page](/gta-6-ultimate-edition) goes through all of
it and says who it suits.

## Pre-order extras in the UK

Every pre-order placed before 20 November 2026 gets the Vintage Vice City
Pack, whichever edition you choose. A digital pre-order also adds one month
of GTA+. Both come at no extra cost.

## What is not confirmed yet

- The UK price of the Ultimate Edition Upgrade on its own.
- Any launch day discount. None has been announced, and we will not guess
  one.
- A PC version or PC price. GTA 6 is only announced for PS5 and Xbox Series
  X|S.
