## The short answer

**GTA 6 costs 79.99 USD for the Standard Edition and 99.99 USD for the
Ultimate Edition.** Those two numbers come straight from Rockstar. Every
other country price below is either matched from an official storefront or
reported by gaming press, and this page tells you which is which.

## Where the prices come from

Rockstar published its US prices on the Newswire, the same post that
confirmed pre-orders would open on 25 June 2026. That is a Tier 1 source. It
is Rockstar's own words, on Rockstar's own site.

Not every country got the same treatment. Some regional prices, like the UK
one, were pulled straight from Rockstar's own storefronts by journalists at
the time pre-orders went live. Those count as confirmed too, even though the
number did not appear on the Newswire post itself.

Other regions, including India, have only ever shown up in press reporting.
No Rockstar page has published those numbers directly. This page marks every
row so you know which kind of source it is.

## Why the price is not the same everywhere

People often assume a game should just cost "the same amount, converted."
That is not how global game pricing works, for three separate reasons.

**Tax.** The US price you see is usually before sales tax, which varies by
state. The UK and EU prices you see are usually tax inclusive, because VAT has
to be shown in the sticker price in those markets. That alone makes a simple
currency conversion look wrong even when nothing shady is happening.

**Currency conversion is not the whole story.** If you convert 79.99 USD to
pounds at a flat exchange rate, you do not land on 69.99 GBP. Publishers round
regional prices to numbers that look normal in that currency, and they do not
recalculate every time the exchange rate moves day to day. A UK price fixed
months ago can end up higher or lower than a live currency conversion, purely
by chance of timing.

**Regional pricing strategy.** Publishers set different price points in
different markets based on what people in that market are used to paying and
what they can afford. This is the same reason a coffee costs a different
amount, adjusted for local wages, in different cities. It is deliberate, and
every major publisher does it, not just Rockstar.

None of this means anyone is being overcharged on purpose. It means "convert
the US price yourself" is not a reliable way to guess another country's price.
Use the confirmed number for your own region where one exists.

## What the Ultimate Edition actually adds

The extra 20 USD (or the equivalent in your currency) for the Ultimate
Edition buys exclusive vehicles, weapons, apparel and businesses, plus two
extra side missions, according to Rockstar's own PlayStation Store listing.
That is the full confirmed list. Nothing else about Ultimate Edition content
has been detailed publicly.

Whether that is worth it depends on how you play. If you plan to explore
everything a Rockstar open world offers and you like having extra vehicles and
outfits from day one, the cosmetic and vehicle bonuses have some value. If you
only care about the main story, the two extra side missions are a small
amount of additional content for the price difference, and the Standard
Edition gets you the entire main game either way. Nobody has played GTA 6 yet,
so nobody, including reviewers, can tell you how substantial those two side
missions actually are in practice.

## The pre-order bonus applies to both editions

Whichever edition you choose, pre-ordering before 20 November 2026 gets you
the Vintage Vice City Pack for free. That includes a 1955 Vapid Stanier
sedan and a garage, access to Ocean Beach, outfits and hairstyles for Jason
and Lucia, and a themed weapon pattern. It costs nothing extra on top of the
edition price. Buying digitally also adds one free month of GTA+.

## What this page will not tell you

We are not going to guess a price for a country that has not been reported
anywhere, and we are not going to round a press-reported number up or down to
make it look tidier. If your country is not on the table above, no reliable
price has surfaced for it yet. Check back closer to launch, since more
regional storefronts are expected to go live before 19 November 2026.

## Digital price versus physical price

Rockstar's confirmed prices apply to the digital versions sold through
PlayStation Store and Xbox Store. Physical copies exist too. Rockstar has
confirmed that physical copies ship with a download code rather than a full
disc, but it has not published a separate physical price that differs from
the digital one. Retailers can and do run their own promotions on physical
stock, so a physical copy might end up cheaper or more expensive than the
digital storefront price depending on where you buy it and when. That is a
retailer decision, not a Rockstar one, and this page cannot predict it.

## How GTA 6 pricing compares with past Rockstar games

GTA 5 launched in 2013 at 59.99 USD. GTA 6 launches at 79.99 USD for the
Standard Edition, a rise of 20 USD across thirteen years. That is a real
increase, but it is smaller in percentage terms than the jump in production
costs the games industry has talked about over the same period. Whether
79.99 USD feels reasonable to you is a personal call. What matters for this
page is that the number is confirmed, not a guess.

The Ultimate Edition sits at 99.99 USD, which is a newer pattern. Past
Rockstar single player releases did not usually come in a two tier pricing
structure with a paid upgrade bundle attached at launch. This is a change in
how the game is being sold, not just a change in how much it costs.

## Checking the price for your own country

If your country is not on the table above, the most reliable way to check is
to look directly at the PlayStation Store or Xbox Store listing for your
region, since those storefronts show the live localised price rather than a
number copied from somewhere else. Gaming press in your region may also
report a number before Rockstar confirms it directly, the way outlets did for
India. Treat those early numbers the same way this page
treats them: real, but not locked in until Rockstar's own channels repeat
them.

## Preloading and paying early

Preloading starts on 12 November 2026, one week before the 19 November 2026
launch, for players who have already pre-ordered digitally. Preloading does
not change the price. It only lets you download the game ahead of time so it
is ready to play the moment it unlocks. You still pay the same confirmed
price for your edition and region whether you preload or not.
