## The short answer

**Grand Theft Auto VI releases on 19 November 2026.** It launches on
PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S on that day, worldwide.

This is not a rumour or a retailer placeholder. Rockstar Games published the
date on its own Newswire on 24 June 2026, one day before pre-orders opened. It
is the same date shown on the PlayStation Store and the Xbox Store listings.

## How Rockstar confirmed it

The date appeared in the Newswire post announcing that pre-orders would open on
25 June 2026. That post also set the prices, named the two editions, and
described the Vintage Vice City pre-order bonus. A publisher naming a date in
the same breath as taking money for it is about as firm as a date gets before
launch.

Take-Two, the company that owns Rockstar, repeated the date publicly afterwards.
Chief executive Strauss Zelnick said on an earnings call that the game would not
be delayed again.

## The two delays, in order

GTA 6 has slipped twice. Both slips were announced by Rockstar or Take-Two
directly, not leaked.

| Target | Announced | What happened |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Autumn 2025 | The original window given for the game | Never hit |
| 26 May 2026 | The first replacement date | Moved again |
| 19 November 2026 | Announced around 6 November 2025 | The current date |

Rockstar gave the same reason both times: it wanted more time to finish the
game to the standard it wanted. Bloomberg reported the second delay and the
reasoning behind it.

## Why a November date makes sense

November is where the biggest games go. It sits in the holiday buying window,
it lands inside Take-Two's financial year, and it gives the game the whole
Christmas period to sell. Moving from May to November also moved the game out of
a quieter sales month into the busiest one.

## What is still not known

Plenty. Be careful with any site that claims otherwise.

- **No unlock time.** Rockstar has not said what hour the game goes live in your
  country. Past Rockstar games unlocked at midnight local time. That is a
  pattern, not a promise.
- **No PC date.** GTA 6 launches on consoles only. Rockstar has not announced a
  PC version at all, let alone dated one. See the platforms page for what is
  actually confirmed.
- **No review date.** Rockstar has not said when reviews or previews go up.
- **No age rating published yet** in most regions.

## What you can do now

Pre-orders have been open since 25 June 2026. Anyone who pre-orders before
20 November 2026 gets the Vintage Vice City pack free, on either edition. If you
buy digitally, you also get one free month of GTA+.

Preloading starts on 12 November 2026, one week before launch, for digital
pre-orders.
