## The short answer

**GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time on 19 November 2026 on PS5**, going by
the PlayStation Store listings. That means it rolls out country by country,
starting in New Zealand. Rockstar has confirmed the date, not the time, and
no Xbox unlock time has been published yet. On launch night, check
[is GTA 6 out yet](/gta-6-launch) for a live answer.

## Who set these times

Be clear about where the times below come from. Rockstar's own Newswire post
gives the release date, 19 November 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. It does
not give an hour.

The hours come from the PlayStation Store. Each regional listing carries a
release timestamp, and gaming outlets including GTA BOOM, Notebookcheck and
PlayStation LifeStyle read those timestamps in early September 2026. They
found 15 separate unlock windows covering 63 countries and regions.

So these are real store times, not guesses. But they are Sony's store
settings, not a Rockstar statement, and they could still change before
launch. Your own store page is the final word.

| New Zealand | 12 a.m. NZDT, 19 Nov | 11:00, 18 Nov |
| Australia (Sydney, Melbourne) | 12 a.m. AEDT, 19 Nov | 13:00, 18 Nov |
| Australia (Perth) | 9 p.m. AWST, 18 Nov | 13:00, 18 Nov |
| Japan and South Korea | 12 a.m. JST/KST, 19 Nov | 15:00, 18 Nov |
| India | 12 a.m. IST, 19 Nov | 18:30, 18 Nov |
| United Arab Emirates | 12 a.m. GST, 19 Nov | 20:00, 18 Nov |
| Europe (Germany, France, Spain, Italy and more) | 12 a.m. CET, 19 Nov | 23:00, 18 Nov |
| United Kingdom and Ireland | 12 a.m. GMT, 19 Nov | 00:00, 19 Nov |
| Brazil (Sao Paulo, Rio) | 12 a.m. BRT, 19 Nov | 03:00, 19 Nov |
| US and Canada, Eastern | 12 a.m. ET, 19 Nov | 05:00, 19 Nov |
| US and Canada, Pacific | 9 p.m. PT, 18 Nov | 05:00, 19 Nov |
| Mexico (Mexico City) | 12 a.m. CST, 19 Nov | 06:00, 19 Nov |

The US and Canada share one store timestamp set to Eastern time. That is why
the west coast gets the game in the evening of 18 November. Central time is
11 p.m. and Mountain time is 10 p.m. the same night. Australia works the same
way, which is why Perth starts at 9 p.m.

Brazil also has more than one time zone. Manaus unlocks at 11 p.m. on 18
November, at the same moment as Sao Paulo's midnight.

## What about Pakistan?

Pakistan does not have its own PlayStation Store, so there is no Pakistan
row in Sony's schedule. Players in Pakistan usually buy through an account
set to another country. The unlock follows that account's store region.

| Your account's store region | Unlock in Pakistan time (PKT) |
| --- | --- |
| United Arab Emirates | 1 a.m., 19 Nov |
| United Kingdom | 5 a.m., 19 Nov |
| United States | 10 a.m., 19 Nov |

These are straight conversions of the store times above. Pakistan time is
UTC+5.

## What about Xbox?

No Xbox unlock time has been published. The Xbox Store confirms 19 November
2026 as the date but does not show the same country by country timestamps
that the PlayStation Store does.

Nobody has said whether Xbox will follow the same midnight windows as PS5.
Do not assume it will. Check your Xbox Store listing in the week before
launch, since its countdown will show the real time once it is set.

## Is a rolling midnight launch normal for Rockstar?

Yes. This is the past pattern, labelled as a pattern. Neither GTA 5 in 2013
nor Red Dead Redemption 2 in 2018 unlocked at one single moment worldwide.
Both reached some regions before others, and Red Dead Redemption 2 opened
first in Australia and New Zealand. GTA 6 on PS5 follows the same idea.

## Preload so you can play at the unlock time

Preloading starts on 12 November 2026, one week before release, for digital
pre-orders. If the game is already downloaded, it can start the moment your
region unlocks. If it is not, you face a large download on launch night,
when servers are at their busiest.

Physical copies are a code in a box, not a disc. Whatever you buy, you still
download the game. See our [GTA 6 preload guide](/gta-6-preload) for the
details.

## Can you play early with a different region?

Not in any simple way. On PlayStation, the unlock follows your account's
country or region. Changing the console clock does nothing. A PlayStation
account's region also cannot be changed after it is made, and Sony expects
it to match your billing details.

Some Xbox players change their console region to try for an earlier unlock.
Nobody has confirmed that this works for GTA 6, and there is no Xbox
schedule yet to test it against. We would not plan launch night around it.
