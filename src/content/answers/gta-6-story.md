## The short answer

**Rockstar has confirmed a premise, not a plot.** Grand Theft Auto VI follows
two leads, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, who get pulled into a criminal
conspiracy across the fictional state of Leonida after a robbery goes wrong.
That is what Rockstar's own site says. Anything more detailed than that,
including most of what circulates online as a "story leak," is not something
Rockstar has confirmed.

## What Rockstar has actually said

Rockstar's official Grand Theft Auto VI site describes the setup this way:
Jason and Lucia have always known the deck is stacked against them, and when
an easy score goes wrong, they end up on the darkest side of the sunniest
place in America, caught in the middle of a criminal conspiracy that
stretches across the state of Leonida. From there, the two of them have to
rely on each other more than ever if they want to make it out alive.

Rockstar's companion page, Only in Leonida, adds background on the two leads
directly. Jason grew up around grifters and crooks, then did a stint in the
Army before ending up in the Leonida Keys working for local drug runners. He
is looking for something better, though it is not going smoothly. Lucia's
father taught her to fight, and life kept coming at her the same way. A fight
to protect her family landed her in Leonida Penitentiary, and she got out on
luck rather than a clean record. She wants the better life her mother always
talked about, and this time she intends to build it herself rather than wait
for it.

Rockstar's site frames the two of them meeting as the center of the story it
is choosing to show the public. Beyond that framing, Rockstar has not
published a plot summary, a mission list, a running time, or an ending.

## Why this page does not repeat leaked material

In September 2022, a hacker who went by "teapotuberhacker" posted roughly 90
videos to the forum GTAForums, showing about 50 minutes of unfinished, work
in progress GTA 6 footage. The material was taken from Rockstar's internal
Slack groups without permission. Take-Two, Rockstar's parent company, filed
takedown notices against the videos and forum threads, and reporting at the
time described it as one of the biggest leaks in video game history. A
17 year old was later arrested and prosecuted in connection with the breach.

That footage still circulates, and a large amount of "GTA 6 story" content
online, including character names, scenes, and plot claims, traces back to
it rather than to anything Rockstar has released since. This site does not
publish or summarize that material as story content, for two reasons.
First, it was stolen, unfinished, work in progress footage, not a
representation of the finished game Rockstar chose to show the public.
Second, treating it as confirmed fact would break the one thing this site is
for, which is telling readers only what has actually been confirmed. Where
the official record and the leak overlap, this page still only cites the
official source.

## What is still not known

- The actual plot beyond the setup Rockstar has described: how Jason and
  Lucia's paths cross, what the "criminal conspiracy" involves in detail,
  and how the story resolves.
- Whether the story is told strictly from both Jason and Lucia's
  perspectives throughout, or how it is structured.
- Any mission names, chapter structure, or story length.
- Whether the story connects in any way to earlier Grand Theft Auto games
  beyond sharing the Vice City name and setting.

## How this page stays honest

Every claim above traces to Rockstar's own Grand Theft Auto VI site or its
Only in Leonida character page, both linked at the foot of this page. The
2022 leak is described here only as a documented event, sourced to a
reference with its own citations, not as a source for plot details. Nothing
from that leaked footage is repeated as story fact on this page, and where
Rockstar has not said something, this page says so instead of guessing.
