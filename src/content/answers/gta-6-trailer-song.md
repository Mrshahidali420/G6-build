## The short answer

**Rockstar has confirmed one trailer song directly, and press outlets have
identified the rest by ear.** Trailer 1 uses Tom Petty's "Love Is a Long
Road." Trailer 2 leads with The Pointer Sisters' "Hot Together," plus three
short radio snippets. The Netflix Extended Look adds thirteen more tracks
across its 26 minutes. None of this is an official Rockstar tracklist, so it
is presented here with each source named.

## Why "confirmed" needs a careful definition here

Rockstar has not published an official soundtrack list for GTA 6. That means
every song below falls into one of two categories, and this page keeps them
separate.

- **Officially confirmed.** The Trailer 1 song was confirmed by Tom Petty's
  own official website, which counts as a primary source since it comes
  directly from the rights holder, not a guess by a journalist.
- **Press reported.** Every other song on this page was identified by a music
  or gaming outlet listening closely to the trailer or the Extended Look and
  matching the audio to a known track. This is common and usually reliable,
  but it is not the same as Rockstar publishing its own tracklist.

Both categories are useful. Neither should be mistaken for the other.

## Trailer 1, December 2023

Trailer 1 uses a single song for its entire runtime.

| Song | Artist | Year | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Love Is a Long Road | Tom Petty | 1989 | Officially confirmed |

"Love Is a Long Road" comes from Tom Petty's 1989 solo debut album, Full Moon
Fever. Tom Petty's official site posted about the placement on the same day
the trailer released, noting that it followed his earlier appearance in GTA:
San Andreas with "Runnin' Down a Dream." The song's lyrics about doing right
on a long road fit the trailer's tone, and Spotify reported streams of the
track rose by roughly 36,979 percent in the week after the trailer went live.

## Trailer 2, May 2026

Trailer 2, released 6 May 2026, uses one lead song for most of its runtime,
plus three short radio snippets during a single driving scene. All four were
identified by NME, not confirmed directly by Rockstar or the artists.

| Song | Artist | Year | Role in the trailer | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hot Together | The Pointer Sisters | 1986 | Leads most of the trailer's runtime | Press reported |
| Everybody Have Fun Tonight | Wang Chung | 1986 | Brief radio snippet during a driving scene | Press reported |
| Child Support | Zenglen with 5 Etwal | 2004 | Brief radio snippet during a driving scene | Press reported |
| Talkin' to Myself Again | Tammy Wynette | 1987 | Brief radio snippet during a driving scene | Press reported |

"Hot Together" underscores a montage that introduces both protagonists,
covering romance, a gunfight, and a helicopter chase through Leonida. NME
placed it starting around the 1:04 mark. The three radio snippets play only
for a few seconds each as protagonist Jason drives, and NME noted the Zenglen
and 5 Etwal track reflects Florida's real-world Haitian community, which fits
Leonida's Florida-inspired setting.

## What this tells you about GTA 6's music direction

Even without an official tracklist, a pattern shows up across all three
pieces of footage. GTA 6 leans on real songs from real decades, mostly the
1970s through the 2020s, rather than only recent chart hits. Trailer 1 and
much of the Extended Look favour older tracks with an emotional or nostalgic
pull, while the driving and action scenes lean on faster, more upbeat music.
That mirrors how past GTA games have used licensed music, mixing eras rather
than sticking to one.

## What is still not known

- No official Rockstar soundtrack or tracklist has been published.
- No confirmation of in-game radio stations or their lineups.
- No confirmation of a third trailer, so there is nothing to say about music
  in footage that does not exist yet.
- The exact original release year for "Skrilla" by Kodak Black is inconsistent
  across music databases, so it is not stated as a fixed fact here.

## How this page stays honest

Every song above is either confirmed by the artist's own channel or traced to
a named press outlet that watched the footage and documented a timestamp. No
song appears on this page unless one of those two things is true. If Rockstar
publishes an official soundtrack list, or a third trailer arrives with new
music, this page will be updated to match.
