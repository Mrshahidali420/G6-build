## The short answer

Rockstar has put out three official Grand Theft Auto VI videos. Two numbered
trailers and one longer film. That is the whole list.

| Video | Released | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Trailer 1 | 4 December 2023 | The first look at Jason, Lucia and Leonida |
| Trailer 2 | 6 May 2025 | The second trailer, released after the first delay |
| An Extended Look | 27 August 2026 | A 26 minute 48 second film, not a trailer |

There is no Trailer 3. Rockstar has not released one and has not said one is
coming.

## How to tell a real trailer from a mirror

This is the part most pages get wrong, and it is easy to get wrong.

A YouTube video ID does not tell you who owns the video. Search "GTA 6 Trailer
1" and you will get the Rockstar upload, an IGN mirror, and a pile of fan
reuploads, all carrying the same footage. Several of them are old, and some sit
on channels that have since renamed themselves.

The check is simple and free. YouTube publishes an oEmbed endpoint that returns
the owning channel for any video ID:

```
https://www.youtube.com/oembed?url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID&format=json
```

The response contains `author_name`. For a genuine Rockstar upload it reads
`Rockstar Games`. Every ID embedded on this page was run through that endpoint
before it was published. Two IDs that other sites list as official failed the
check and were dropped: one belongs to IGN, one to a fan channel.
