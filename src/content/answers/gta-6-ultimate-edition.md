## The short answer

**GTA 6 Ultimate Edition costs 99.99 USD, 20 USD more than the 79.99 USD
Standard Edition.** For that extra money, Rockstar confirms you get exclusive
vehicles, weapons, apparel and businesses, plus two extra side missions. Both
editions contain the full main game.

| | Standard Edition | Ultimate Edition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price (USD) | 79.99 | 99.99 |
| Price (UK, GBP) | 69.99 | 89.99 |
| Full main story | Included | Included |

| Platforms | PS5, Xbox Series X\|S | PS5, Xbox Series X\|S |

The row that matters most is the top one. Standard Edition is not a stripped
down or limited version of GTA 6. It is the full game Rockstar built. Ultimate
Edition is that same game with a bundle of extras stacked on top.

## What the extra 20 USD actually buys

Rockstar's own PlayStation Store listing is specific about this, and it is
worth quoting exactly rather than guessing. Ultimate Edition adds:

- **Exclusive vehicles.** Extra cars, bikes or similar available to Ultimate
  Edition owners that are not part of the Standard Edition.
- **Exclusive weapons.** Additional weapons on top of the base game's
  weapon set.
- **Exclusive apparel.** Extra clothing and cosmetic items for your
  characters.
- **Exclusive businesses.** Additional in-world businesses beyond what
  Standard Edition players get.
- **Two extra side missions.** Additional optional content beyond the main
  story, which is identical across both editions.

Rockstar has since named every item in the bundle. The full list is further
down, under "Is the Ultimate Edition worth it?". What nobody can tell you yet is how long the extra content
takes to play, because nobody outside Rockstar has played the game.

## Don't confuse this with the Vintage Vice City Pack

It is easy to mix up the Ultimate Edition bonuses with the separate pre-order
bonus, so it is worth being precise here.

The **Vintage Vice City Pack** is not an Ultimate Edition exclusive. It comes
free with a pre-order of either edition, Standard or Ultimate, as long as you
pre-order before 20 November 2026. It contains a 1955 Vapid Stanier sedan and
a garage, access to Ocean Beach, outfits and hairstyles for Jason and Lucia,
and a themed weapon pattern.

So a Standard Edition pre-order gets the Vintage Vice City Pack too. Buying
Ultimate does not double up on that specific bonus. It adds a separate,
larger set of exclusive content on top of whichever edition you already
bought.

## The GTA+ free month

Both editions come with the same GTA+ benefit if you pre-order digitally: one
free month of the subscription. This is not an Ultimate Edition exclusive
either. It applies the same way regardless of which edition you choose,
provided the purchase is digital.

Buying physically does not carry the same guarantee, since Rockstar has only
confirmed the free month for digital pre-orders specifically.

## Is the Ultimate Edition worth it?

Short verdict: **worth it if you plan to live in Leonida for months, skip it
if you are there for the story.** The main game is the same in both
editions. And because Rockstar says the upgrade can be bought separately
later, picking Standard now does not lock you out.

### What do you get extra in the Ultimate Edition?

Rockstar's full list, as reported by Stevivor and Press Start on the day
pre-orders opened, is all single-player content for Jason and Lucia:

- **Vehicles:** the '95 Grotti Cheetah, the '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy with
  the Paradise Garage, the Shitzu Squalo boat, a Dinka Enduro motorcycle or
  Crest Kayak at Jason's safehouse, and a Vapid Ganado Retro Build.
- **Classic Car Collection:** a job from a collector called Wyman to restore
  old cars, including four that only exist in the Ultimate Edition.
- **Two vehicle mod shops:** Rideout Customs in Vice City and One-Eyed
  Willie's in Lake Leonida, open only in the Ultimate Edition.
- **Weapons:** his and hers Hawk Little Morgan Revolvers, plus engraved
  versions of Jason's and Lucia's pistols.
- **A gang raid:** the PTT Youngin$ Illegal Goods Store in Southside Vice
  City, with special items to score.
- **Looks:** Vice City outfits and tattoos, the Goodtime Gear clothing line,
  the Stock 305 clothing store, Sara's Unisex Salon, and Electric Fang
  Tattoo with over 50 exclusive tattoos.

Rockstar also said these items unlock across the story, chapter by chapter.
So you do not get everything on day one. It is spread through the game.

### Will the Ultimate Edition include online?

No online content has been announced for it. Every item above is for Jason
and Lucia in the single-player game. Take-Two's press release calls GTA 6 a
single-player experience, and GamesRadar+ read that as a sign a new GTA
Online will not arrive at launch. Rockstar has not detailed any GTA 6 online
mode. So do not buy the Ultimate Edition expecting online extras. Nothing
says they carry over.

### Should you buy the Ultimate Edition?

**Buy it if** you like to explore, collect cars, mod vehicles and dress up
your characters. Most of the extra money goes on exactly those things: cars,
two mod shops, a clothing store, a salon and a tattoo parlour.

**Skip it if** you want to play the story once and move on. You get the
whole story with Standard. The extras are nice, but most are cosmetic or
convenience items.

**Not sure?** Buy Standard. Rockstar said the Ultimate Edition Upgrade will
be sold separately to Standard owners at any time. The upgrade price has not
been announced, so it could cost more than the 20 USD gap you pay today. That
is the one risk of waiting.

Neither choice changes the free Vintage Vice City Pack or the free month of
GTA+. Both come from pre-ordering, not from the edition. For every item in
detail, see the [full Ultimate Edition record](/editions/gta-6-ultimate-edition).

## Should you upgrade later instead of buying Ultimate now

You can. Rockstar said the Grand Theft Auto VI: Ultimate Edition Upgrade
will be available for Standard Edition owners to buy separately at any time.
The PlayStation Store already lists the upgrade as an add-on, though it
cannot be pre-ordered yet.

What Rockstar has not given is the price of that upgrade on its own. It may
match the 20 USD gap between the editions, or it may not. If you already know
you want the extras, buying Ultimate now is the only way to lock in today's
price. If you are unsure, buying Standard and waiting for reviews is a safe
choice, since the upgrade stays on sale.

## Who Ultimate Edition is aimed at

Going by what Rockstar has confirmed, the Ultimate Edition content leans
towards players who spend a lot of time inside the open world doing more than
just the main story, since exclusive vehicles, weapons, apparel and
businesses only matter if you are out exploring and using them. A player who
mostly wants to finish the story once and move on gets identical access to
that story with the Standard Edition, and would likely get the least value
out of the extra 20 USD.

## What we still do not know

Rockstar has named every Ultimate Edition item, but not the price of the
Ultimate Edition Upgrade bought on its own, or how long the extra content
takes to play. It has not said whether any of it will carry into a future
online mode. No age rating, file size, or map detail has been published for
either edition. This page will be updated the moment Rockstar shares more.
