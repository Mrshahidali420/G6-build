## The short answer

**Yes, GTA 6 goes back to Vice City.** Rockstar confirmed this on the
official GTA VI site. Vice City is the main city, and it sits inside a
larger fictional state called Leonida. See the full
[Vice City location entry](/locations/vice-city) for every confirmed
detail on the city in one place.

## What Rockstar has actually said

Rockstar's official GTA VI site names Vice City directly as the central
setting of the game. It describes the neon-soaked streets of Vice City and
ties the city to the story of the two leads, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos.
This is not a leak or a fan theory. It is on Rockstar's own site, linked at
the bottom of this page.

Vice City is not the entire game world by itself. It is the biggest and most
central place, sitting inside the wider state of Leonida. Rockstar's
official material also names several other destinations within Leonida,
each with its own dedicated official screenshots and artwork.

## Vice City and the state of Leonida

Leonida is the fictional state that GTA 6 takes place in. Vice City is its
main city, but Leonida is bigger than the city alone. Rockstar has officially
named these places as part of Leonida:

| Place | What it is | Confirmed status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vice City | The main city, the central urban setting | Officially confirmed |
| Leonida Keys | A tropical island chain, linked to Jason's criminal work | Officially confirmed |
| Port Gellhorn | A worn down Gulf Coast area with cheap tourism and roadside businesses | Officially confirmed |
| Grassrivers | A wetland region away from the city | Officially confirmed |
| Ambrosia | An inland industrial area | Officially confirmed |
| Mount Kalaga National Park | A northern wilderness and national park | Officially confirmed |

All six of these come from Rockstar's own official screenshot and artwork
library, each with its own dedicated set of images or a postcard. Rockstar
has not published a full map, exact boundaries, a complete list of
neighbourhoods, or the total size of Leonida or Vice City. Any page that
gives you a precise size in square kilometres for GTA 6 is guessing, because
Rockstar has not released that number.

## A new Vice City, not the old one

Grand Theft Auto: Vice City came out in 2002, on PlayStation 2. It was set
in a version of the city built around a 1980s theme, with a story about a
mobster named Tommy Vercetti. That game is a fan favourite and a big part of
why the name Vice City means so much to people who have followed the series
for a long time.

GTA 6 is not that game rebuilt. It is a brand new title with its own story,
its own two leads, and its own take on the city. Rockstar is reusing the
Vice City name and the general idea of a neon, Florida-inspired city, not
reusing the 2002 game's map, characters or plot. Anyone expecting a direct
remake of the 2002 game should adjust that expectation now. GTA 6 stands on
its own.

The link between the two games is real, but it is a link of theme and
identity, not of shared content. Vice City has represented Rockstar's
fictional version of Miami since 2002, and GTA 6 continues that same idea
with a modern, current-generation game built around it.

## Why Rockstar keeps returning to Vice City

Vice City has been part of the Grand Theft Auto series in more than one
game. The 2002 title put it on the map for a generation of players, and its
1980s look, its music, and its story stuck with people long after the
console it launched on stopped being sold. Rockstar has also referenced
Vice City in other ways across the years since, which is part of why the
name carries so much weight with longtime fans.

Bringing Vice City back as the main setting for a brand new mainline game is
a different decision from a simple nostalgia nod. It puts the city at the
centre of a full, current-generation Grand Theft Auto title, not a side
reference or an easter egg. Rockstar's official material treats the city as
the heart of the GTA VI world, tied directly to the story of Jason Duval and
Lucia Caminos.

## How Leonida's other destinations relate to the city

Vice City sits at the centre of Leonida, but the other named destinations
each bring a different kind of environment. Leonida Keys is described in
Rockstar's own material as tropical and coastal, linked to Jason Duval's
criminal work and to a character named Brian Heder, who is described as a
drug runner operating in that area. Port Gellhorn is shown as a worn down
Gulf Coast area built around cheap tourism and roadside businesses.

Grassrivers is a wetland region, visually distinct from the dense streets of
the city. Ambrosia is described as an inland industrial area, giving the
state a working class setting away from both the coast and the city centre.
Mount Kalaga National Park rounds out the confirmed list as a northern
wilderness area, Rockstar's official screenshot library includes six images
specifically labelled for it. Together these six confirmed places give
Leonida a range of environments well beyond Vice City's own streets, even
though Vice City remains the game's central and best known location.

## What is still not known about Vice City in GTA 6

Rockstar has not published a full city map, a neighbourhood by neighbourhood
breakdown, or a list of every building and interior. Community made maps and
guesses exist all over the internet, but none of them come from Rockstar,
and they should not be treated as confirmed. This page sticks to what
Rockstar has actually said and nothing more.
