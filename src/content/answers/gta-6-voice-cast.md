## The short answer

**Rockstar has not confirmed who voices Jason or Lucia.** As of mid September
2026, exactly one actor has confirmed his own involvement in Grand Theft Auto
VI: Stephen Root, best known for voicing Bill Dauterive and Buck Strickland on
King of the Hill. He said so himself, in an interview, not Rockstar. Every
other name attached to the GTA 6 cast online is a guess, and this page treats
it that way.

| Lucia Caminos | Not known | Unconfirmed |
| Brian Heder | Stephen Root | Confirmed by the actor himself |

## Press reported

There is nothing to put in this section that meets this site's bar. Every

Rockstar. Rather than repeat an unsourced name here under a softer label, this
page leaves the section empty and says why.

## How to tell a confirmed casting from a rumour

This is the part worth being careful about, because GTA 6 cast lists are one
of the most guessed-at topics anywhere in the coverage.

- **A confirmed casting has a name attached to a quote.** Stephen Root did not
  get added to a wiki table by a fan. He told the Associated Press, on the
  record, "Yeah, you're gonna see me in it." That is a primary source: the
  actor speaking for himself.
- **A rumour usually has no primary source at all.** It spreads through voice
  matching by ear, screenshots of old casting-call listings, or IMDb pages
  that anyone can edit. None of those are the same as an actor or Rockstar
  making a statement.
- **Even a real confirmation can carry an unconfirmed detail.** Root confirmed
  he is in the game. The specific character he plays, Brian Heder, was worked
  out by fans and reporters matching his voice to footage, and Root's comments
  did not deny it. That is a reasonable inference from a named source, not the
  same as Root or Rockstar naming the character outright, so it is presented
  here with that distinction kept visible.
- **A long, detailed cast list with no sources is a warning sign, not a
  reassurance.** The more precise and complete an unsourced list looks, the
  more likely it has been copied and expanded from other unsourced lists.

## What is still not known

- Who voices Jason Duval.
- Who voices Lucia Caminos.
- Whether Rockstar plans to release an official cast list before launch, or
  only after release through the game's credits.
- Whether any other trailer or Extended Look character has a confirmed actor
  beyond Stephen Root as Brian Heder.

## How this page stays honest

This page lists exactly one confirmed voice actor, because that is what the
sourced record shows. Rockstar's own silence on the cast is treated as a fact
worth stating plainly, not worked around with a long unsourced list borrowed
from elsewhere. If Rockstar names an actor, or another cast member confirms
their own role the way Stephen Root did, this page will be updated with that
source named.
