## The short answer

GTA 5 is a finished, eleven-year-old game with a full public history. GTA 6 is
an unreleased game with a small set of facts confirmed by Rockstar. Comparing
them fairly means being honest about which GTA 6 numbers simply do not exist
yet.

## Why this comparison is hard to do properly

Most "GTA 6 vs GTA 5" pages compare a game people have played for over a
decade against a game nobody has played at all. That is not a fair
comparison, and it is easy to fill the GTA 6 side with guesses dressed up as
facts.

This page does not do that. Everything said about GTA 5 below is settled
history. Everything said about GTA 6 comes from Rockstar's own Newswire and
official site. Where GTA 6 has no public answer, the table says so plainly
instead of guessing.

## GTA 5: the short history

Grand Theft Auto V released in September 2013 on PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360.
It was set in Los Santos, a fictional stand in for Los Angeles, inside the
wider fictional state of San Andreas. The story followed three playable
leads, Michael, Franklin and Trevor, who could be switched between at almost
any time.

Rockstar then brought GTA 5 forward across a long run of hardware. It came to
PS4 and Xbox One in 2014, to PC in 2015, and later to PS5 and Xbox Series
X|S. That slow, repeated re-release across nearly every generation of console
since 2013 is part of why GTA 5 sold as many copies as it did. It is one of
the best selling entertainment products ever made, across any medium.

GTA Online, the multiplayer mode built on top of GTA 5, launched a few weeks
after the main game and kept being updated for over a decade. That long
online life is a separate part of the GTA 5 story, and it matters when
people ask what might happen with a GTA 6 online mode. That question gets
its own page, because there is no confirmed answer to it yet.

## GTA 6: what Rockstar has actually confirmed

GTA 6 is set in the fictional state of Leonida, with Vice City as its main
city. The two lead characters are Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. Rockstar
confirmed both of these facts on its official GTA VI site.

The release date is 19 November 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
Rockstar confirmed that date on its own Newswire on 24 June 2026, the day
before pre-orders opened. No PC version has been announced or dated.

The game has been delayed twice. The original target was autumn 2025. That
moved to 26 May 2026, and then moved again to 19 November 2026, in an
announcement made around 6 November 2025. Both delays were announced directly
by Rockstar or Take-Two, not leaked.

are gaps in what Rockstar has said, and that is worth stating directly rather
than filling in with a guess.

## Setting: Los Santos versus Vice City

GTA 5's Los Santos drew on Los Angeles and southern California. GTA 6 moves
the series back toward Florida, with Vice City as its main city inside the
larger state of Leonida. Rockstar's official material also names several
other Leonida destinations beyond the city itself, including Leonida Keys,
Port Gellhorn, Grassrivers, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. That is
a wider confirmed set of named locations than Rockstar gave for GTA 5's San
Andreas at any single point before launch.

## Characters: three leads versus two

GTA 5's three character system, switching between Michael, Franklin and
Trevor, was a headline feature at the time. GTA 6 drops that structure to two
leads, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. Rockstar has not said whether players
will switch between them the same way GTA 5 allowed, or how that mechanic
will work. That detail sits outside the confirmed facts list, so this page
will not guess at it.

## Platforms: a similar pattern, an unclear ending

GTA 5's platform story ran for years, arriving on new hardware each time a
new console generation showed up. GTA 6 is following a similar opening move,
launching on the current console generation, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Where
the pattern breaks down is PC. GTA 5 eventually reached PC. GTA 6 has no PC
version confirmed, dated, or priced. It may follow the same path GTA 5 did.
It may not. Nobody outside Rockstar knows yet, and treating "eventually" as a
promise would be dishonest.

## Price: a fixed number versus a changing one

GTA 5 launched at a normal retail price for 2013 and its price changed many
times over the following decade, through sales, bundles, and re-releases on
newer consoles. GTA 6 has a fixed launch price that Rockstar has already
published. The standard edition is 79.99 USD and the ultimate edition is
99.99 USD. The ultimate edition adds exclusive vehicles, weapons, apparel and
businesses, plus two extra side missions, on top of the standard game.

Pricing outside the United States also differs by region. In the United
Kingdom, GTA 6 is priced at GBP 69.99 for the standard edition and GBP 89.99
for the ultimate edition. In Europe, the prices reported are EUR 79.99 and
EUR 99.99. In Canada the reported prices are CAD 109.99 and CAD 139.99, and
in Australia AUD 129.95 and AUD 159.95. These regional figures come from
press reporting rather than Rockstar's own Newswire, so they carry slightly
lower confidence than the US dollar prices Rockstar published directly.

## Pre-order bonuses: a new idea for the series

GTA 5 did not launch with the kind of structured pre-order bonus system GTA
6 has. Rockstar has confirmed that anyone who pre-orders GTA 6 before 20
November 2026 gets the Vintage Vice City pack for free, on either edition.
That pack includes a 1955 Vapid Stanier sedan and a garage, access to Ocean
Beach, outfits and hairstyles for both Jason and Lucia, and a themed weapon
pattern. Digital pre-orders also come with one free month of GTA+, Rockstar's
subscription service.

None of this existed in the same form around GTA 5's launch. It reflects how
much the way publishers sell games has changed in the years between the two
titles, more than it reflects anything about the games themselves.

## Development gap: over a decade apart

GTA 5 released in September 2013. GTA 6 releases on 19 November 2026. That
gap is more than thirteen years, the longest wait between mainline Grand
Theft Auto releases in the series' history. Most of that gap was filled by
GTA Online's long run on top of GTA 5, which is likely one reason Rockstar
kept supporting GTA 5 for as long as it did instead of moving straight to a
new game.

A long gap between games is not unusual for Rockstar. It followed a similar
pattern between GTA 4 in 2008 and GTA 5 in 2013, though that gap was shorter.
What makes the GTA 5 to GTA 6 gap unusual is simply its length, driven partly
by two public delays that pushed the original autumn 2025 target out to
November 2026.

## What this page will not do

It will not guess a map size for GTA 6. It will not guess whether GTA Online
continues after GTA 6 launches. It will not guess a PC date. It will not
compare mission counts, vehicle counts, or content volume between the two
games, because GTA 6 has not released and nobody has played it.
