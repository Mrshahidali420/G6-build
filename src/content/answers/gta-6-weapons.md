## The short answer

**Rockstar has not published a weapons list for GTA 6.** What exists instead
is a set of weapons that have actually been seen on screen, in Trailer 1,
Trailer 2, official screenshots, or the Netflix Extended Look. Some carry
proper names, like the ES9 and DW9. Others have not been named and are
identified only by the real firearm they resemble. Nothing below is a stat, a
price, or an unlock condition, because Rockstar has not published any of
those either.

## Why some names are missing

A number of weapons above have no proper in-game name yet. Rockstar has shown
the weapon clearly enough to identify the real firearm it is modelled on, a
Ruger 10/22 or a Heckler and Koch MP5 for example, but has not shown or stated
what Rockstar itself will call it in the finished game. Past GTA games have
sometimes used plain descriptive names instead of real gun names, so a final
name may differ from the real-world weapon it resembles.

## Weapons that are not on this list

Material described as leaked, rather than shown in an official trailer,
screenshot, or the Extended Look, is left off this page on purpose. GTA Wiki's
own weapons record separately marks items such as a Rocket Launcher, a
Speargun, Flashbangs, Golf Balls, and Grenades as coming only from leaked
material, not from anything Rockstar has released. Those items may well be
real, but they have not been officially shown, so they do not belong on a page
that only covers what has actually been seen.

## What is still not known

- Whether every weapon above will keep the name or design shown in pre-release
  material once the game ships.
- Any damage values, fire rates, ammo capacity, or unlock requirements. None
  of that has been published for any weapon.
- Whether a fuller weapons list, official or otherwise, will appear before the
  19 November 2026 release.
- Whether weapons seen only in leaked material, such as a rocket launcher or
  grenades, will be confirmed through an official trailer before launch.

## How this page stays honest

Every weapon listed here has a specific piece of official material behind it,
a trailer, a screenshot, or the Extended Look, drawn from GTA Wiki's own
weapons gallery, which by its stated policy excludes leaked content, and
cross-checked against IGN's separately compiled weapons list. No stat, price,
or unlock detail appears anywhere on this page, because none has been
published. Where a weapon is known only from leaked material and not from
anything Rockstar has shown, it is named in the section above as excluded,
not folded into the main list.
