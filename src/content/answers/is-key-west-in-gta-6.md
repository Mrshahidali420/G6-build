## The short answer

**Rockstar has not named Key West, or any version of it, in GTA 6.** What it
has confirmed is the Leonida Keys, a tropical chain of islands in the state
of Leonida. They are plainly modelled on the real Florida Keys. Fan mappers have matched
a handful of in-game spots to real Key West places, but that is their work,
not Rockstar's.

## What Rockstar has confirmed

Rockstar's official GTA VI site and its Only in Leonida page name the
[Leonida Keys](/locations/leonida-keys) as one of the game's regions. Here is
what that official material shows:

- Low islands linked by causeways, with boats, bars and beaches.
- Jason Duval works in the Keys.
- A drug runner called Brian Heder operates there.
- The Keys first showed up in Trailer 2 on 6 May 2025.

What Rockstar has not published matters just as much. There is no official
list of islands, no island names beyond "Leonida Keys", and no map of the
towns and roads. There is also no town Rockstar has called Key West.

## What fans have mapped

The GTADB community map pairs in-game landmarks with real addresses. Its
public data, fetched on 23 September 2026, holds 14 landmarks whose real
match is in Key West. Ten carry a Key West town address. Four carry a Naval
Air Station Key West address.

A few examples from that map:

- A control tower, radar tower and hangar in an area the map calls Mariana
  County, matched to Naval Air Station Key West.
- A bar called Blue Crab Bar & Grill, matched to Blue Heaven on Thomas
  Street. The map tags this match as unconfirmed.
- A lighthouse matched to Sand Key Lighthouse, also tagged unconfirmed.

The same map places many more spots in the Upper Keys. It counts 30
landmarks matched to Key Largo and 17 to Islamorada.

Read these as fan analogues. A match means someone saw a building in a
trailer or screenshot and found the real place it looks like. Rockstar has
never published a single one of these pairings. Our
[real places page](/real-places) lists the matches row by row, and the
[GTA 6 map](/map) shows where they sit.

## The Key West rumours

Two Reddit posts from 20 September 2026 started the recent talk. We track
both on the rumour tracker:

- [A fan concept map of a "removed" Key West](/tracker/gta-6-community-artist-reveals-removed-key-west-map-concept).
  This is a fan-made drawing. It is not a leak, and it does not prove
  anything was cut.
- [A theory that Key West is still there, just smaller](/tracker/gta-6-is-key-west-still-on-the-map-albeit-smaller).
  It leans on old, incomplete fan maps and on how big airports look in the
  new footage. It is a guess, not evidence.

Neither post has any backing from Rockstar.

## So, is Key West in GTA 6?

The honest answer is "not confirmed". The island chain is in. Some of what
fans have spotted looks a lot like Key West, especially the naval air
station. But Rockstar has not named the town, and nobody outside Rockstar
has seen the full map.

We will know for sure on 19 November 2026, when the game comes out. This
page will be updated if Rockstar names a Key West stand in before then.
