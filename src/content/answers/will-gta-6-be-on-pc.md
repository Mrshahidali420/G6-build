## The short answer

**Rockstar has not announced a PC version of GTA 6.** The confirmed platforms
are PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and only those two.

That is the whole confirmed answer. Everything past this point is either
background on how Rockstar has handled PC releases before, or a plain
statement of what nobody knows yet. Treat the two separately, because a lot of
sites blur them together.

## What Rockstar has confirmed

The Rockstar Newswire post that opened pre-orders on 25 June 2026 named the
release date, the prices, and the platforms. Consoles were the only platforms
listed. GameSpot's coverage of preloading and physical copies repeats the same
point: GTA 6 launches on consoles only, with no PC version announced.

There is no PC pre-order page. There is no PC listing on Steam, the Epic Games
Store, or any other storefront. There is no PC box art, no PC trailer, and no
PC system requirements page. None of that exists because Rockstar has not
said a PC version is coming.

## The past pattern, clearly labelled as a pattern

Every mainline Grand Theft Auto game this century has reached PC eventually.
GTA IV, GTA V, and the earlier games in the series all launched on consoles
first and arrived on PC months or years later. GTA V in particular launched on
PS3 and Xbox 360 in 2013, came to PS4 and Xbox One in 2014, and only reached
PC in April 2015, about a year and a half after the original console launch.

That is a real pattern in Rockstar's history. It is not a promise about GTA 6.
Rockstar has not said "PC will follow later" for this game. The company has
said nothing about PC at all. Readers should treat the pattern as useful
context for what Rockstar has tended to do, not as evidence about what it will
do this time. A company can break its own pattern, and until Rockstar speaks,
neither outcome is confirmed.

It is worth being honest about why sites lean on this pattern so heavily. A
past habit is the only thing available to write about when there is no
announcement. That makes it tempting to write it up as if it were close to
certain. It is not. Treat any PC date you see attached to GTA 6 right now as
someone's guess dressed up as news.

## Why Rockstar might wait to announce PC

Rockstar has a habit of focusing marketing and sales energy on the console
launch first, then opening up a second wave of coverage and sales around a
later PC release. Console-first strategies also let Rockstar and its
publisher, Take-Two, sell the game twice to some of the same players, since
people who buy on console sometimes buy again on PC. None of this is
confirmed for GTA 6 specifically. It is simply the pattern the company has
followed before, offered here as background, not as a prediction.

There is no confirmed reason for a PC version to be delayed, sped up, or
skipped entirely for GTA 6. Nobody outside Rockstar knows the plan, and
Rockstar has not shared one.

## What this means if you only own a PC

Right now, a PC player has no confirmed way to play GTA 6 on release day,
19 November 2026 or afterwards, because no PC version exists yet. If a PC
release does happen, based on the pattern of past games it would likely come
well after the console launch, not alongside it. That is a reasonable
expectation based on history, not a fact anyone can point to.

Do not pre-order anything claiming to be a "GTA 6 PC edition." No such product
has been announced by Rockstar or listed on any official storefront. Anything
selling under that name before an official announcement should be treated
with suspicion.

## System requirements: nothing is published

No PC system requirements exist for GTA 6, because no PC version has been
confirmed. Any list of minimum or recommended specs circulating online,
including graphics card names, RAM numbers, or storage size, is a guess or a
fabrication. Rockstar has not published anything of the kind. This page will
not repeat those guesses, because guessing specs for a version of the game
that has not been announced is not useful information.

## What to watch for

The clearest sign a PC version is coming would be an official Rockstar
Newswire post, a listing on Steam or the Epic Games Store, or a statement from
Take-Two on an earnings call. None of those have happened as of this page's
last update. If Rockstar announces a PC version, this page will be updated
with the date, the platforms, and the price, sourced the same way every other
fact on this site is sourced.

## Why this page will not guess a date

Some sites take the GTA V pattern, count forward from the console launch, and
print a specific month and year for a supposed GTA 6 PC release. That number
is not a fact. It is arithmetic performed on a pattern, dressed up to look
like a report. This page will not do that, because a made-up date is worse
than no date at all. It gives a false sense of certainty about something
Rockstar has not said a single word on.

The honest position is simple. A PC version is plausible, based entirely on
what Rockstar has done with every previous GTA release. Plausible is not the
same as planned, and it is a long way from confirmed. Until Rockstar
publishes something, in a Newswire post, a storefront listing, or a
statement from Take-Two, there is no PC release to report on.

## How to check for yourself

If you want to verify the current state of things rather than take this page
at its word, the most reliable places to look are Rockstar's own Newswire,
the official Steam and Epic Games Store pages for the publisher, and
Take-Two's investor earnings calls, where executives sometimes confirm plans
ahead of a formal marketing push. As of this page's last update, none of
those sources mention a PC version of GTA 6 in any form.

## The bottom line

GTA 6 is confirmed for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S on 19 November 2026, and
nothing else. A PC version is likely eventually, going only by what Rockstar
has done with every past GTA game, but it is not confirmed, not dated, and not
detailed in any way. Anyone telling you otherwise is guessing.
