## Not every brand here is equally solid

Grand Theft Auto has always built its world out of fictional brands standing in for real ones, and GTA 6 is no different. What matters on this page is that these brands are not all confirmed the same way. Some were seen directly in trailer footage. Others are returning names from earlier GTA games that press coverage assumes will carry over, without a confirmed GTA 6 appearance yet. This page keeps that distinction clear rather than treating every name as equally certain.

## The vehicle makers seen in trailer footage

Three vehicle brands have the strongest evidence behind them, because vehicles carrying their badges were directly identified in Grand Theft Auto VI's own trailers. Vapid, the series' long-running stand-in for mainstream American car manufacturing, appears on muscle cars, pickups, and sedans across both trailers. Grotti, the series' Ferrari-style Italian exotic brand, was identified on cars like the Grotti Cheetah in scenes set around Vice City's beachfront strip. Shitzu, associated with motorcycles and marine vehicles such as jet skis, was referenced in press analysis of the game's vehicle culture, fitting naturally given how much of Vice City sits on water. All three are grounded in actual trailer footage, not just a returning name from an older game.

## A kayak brand from a screenshot

Crest is a smaller case. It was identified from an official Grand Theft Auto VI screenshot and a promotional postcard video tied to Mount Kalaga National Park, where a Crest-branded kayak appears. That is a genuine sighting in official material, but it comes from a single screenshot rather than a trailer, and the identification itself was made by community sources rather than stated outright by Rockstar, so it carries a bit less certainty than Vapid, Grotti, or Shitzu.

## Names carried over from earlier games

Three more brands, Sprunk, eCola, and Ammu-Nation, are worth flagging directly, because their situation is genuinely different from the vehicle manufacturers above. All three are long-running names from earlier Grand Theft Auto games. Sprunk and eCola are the series' rival soda brands, echoing the real Coca-Cola and Pepsi rivalry, and both have appeared on vending machines and billboards in GTA 6 trailer footage. Ammu-Nation, the series' recurring gun store chain, showed up in trailer footage as an in-universe television advert for a location branded Phil's Ammu-Nation. So these three do have a confirmed GTA 6 sighting each, but because they are pre-existing series brands rather than new ones built specifically for this game, their appearance carries a slightly different kind of certainty than a brand-new manufacturer shown fresh in this game's own marketing. Read them as confirmed to appear, but as older, returning names rather than newly established ones.

## A coffee brand with the weakest evidence

Bean Machine sits at the bottom of this list. It comes from fan-maintained item databases rather than a direct Rockstar statement or clearly legible trailer signage, and it carries the lowest confidence of any brand on this page. It is included here because it does turn up in the available research, but it should be treated as the least certain entry, not as settled fact.

## What this page will not claim

This page will not tell you every brand exists in the finished game the way it appeared in a trailer, and it will not invent a backstory for any of them beyond what Rockstar or credible press coverage has actually said. As more trailers and previews arrive before the 19 November 2026 launch, expect this list to grow and some of today's lower-confidence entries to get firmer sourcing.
