## Two very different kinds of business

The businesses Rockstar has confirmed for Grand Theft Auto VI split into two groups that work in different ways. Most of them are storefronts tied specifically to the Ultimate Edition, meaning only players who buy that edition, or upgrade to it, can use them. One is different: a record label woven into the story itself, available to everyone regardless of which edition they bought. It helps to know which is which before assuming any of these businesses will be open to every player.

## The Ultimate Edition storefronts

Rockstar published a breakdown of Ultimate Edition bonus content ahead of pre-orders opening on 25 June 2026, and five businesses came out of that breakdown. Rideout Customs is a Vice City mod shop offering custom rims, interiors, and donk-style builds. One-Eyed Willie's is a separate mod shop in Lake Leonida focused on off-road vehicles and hand-painted custom art. Sara's Unisex Salon offers hair and beauty styles for both Jason and Lucia, including facial hair options for Jason and makeup and nail options for Lucia, though Rockstar's own material leaves it unclear whether the whole salon or only these specific styles are Ultimate Edition exclusive. Stock 305 is a streetwear store in the Stockyard neighbourhood, and Electric Fang Tattoo, also in Stockyard, offers more than fifty tattoo designs created by FAILE, a real Brooklyn-based artist collective, which is one of the more unusual pieces of real-world crossover Rockstar has confirmed for this game.

A sixth Ultimate Edition item sits slightly apart from the rest. Goodtime Gear is a themed clothing and accessories collection inspired by Macca the Gator, a fictional television character within the game's world, rather than a walk-in shop like the others.

## The one you fight to get into

PTT Youngin$ is different again. Rockstar describes it as a gang-controlled compound in Southside Vice City that Ultimate Edition owners can raid to get exclusive contraband and items. It is not a shop you walk into and buy from, it is a location you fight your way into, and its exact name spelling has actually varied between press sources covering Rockstar's own announcement, which is why this page notes that inconsistency rather than picking one spelling as definitive.

## The business that belongs to everyone

Only Raw Records stands apart from the other seven. It is a hip hop record label and recording studio co-founded by two named characters, Dre'Quan Priest and Boobie Ike, and it is part of the story itself rather than an edition bonus. Rockstar's own character biographies describe Dre'Quan running the creative side while Boobie funds it through his strip club earnings, and the label's first signed act is the rap duo Real Dimez. Because this business is tied to the story and the characters rather than to a purchase tier, it should not be confused with the six Ultimate Edition storefronts above it.

## What is still unclear

Rockstar has not said how any of these businesses actually function in gameplay terms, what anything costs, or how often their offerings change. The salon's exact scope of exclusivity is openly uncertain even in Rockstar's own material, and the compound's name has been spelled two different ways in press coverage of the same announcement. None of that should be read as a full picture of Leonida's economy, just the specific businesses Rockstar has put a name to so far.
