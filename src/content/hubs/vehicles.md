## What this page actually covers

Every vehicle Rockstar has officially named for Grand Theft Auto VI so far is a bonus item tied to an edition or a pre-order, not a vehicle pulled from the finished game's full roster. Rockstar's own edition page and its screenshot library are the source for each one. That matters, because it means this page cannot yet answer "what cars are in GTA 6", only "what cars has Rockstar shown us up to launch".

## The Ultimate Edition vehicles

Buying the Ultimate Edition of Grand Theft Auto VI, priced at 99.99 USD, comes with a set of vehicles Rockstar lists directly on its edition page. These include the '95 Grotti Cheetah, a mid nineties sports car from the fictional Grotti brand, the '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy, a 1967-styled off-road buggy that comes with its own garage, and the Shitzu Squalo, a watercraft. Jason also gets a set of Safehouse Vehicles as part of the same edition, which include the Dinka Enduro, a motorcycle, and the Crest Kayak, a small paddle craft. Rockstar's official screenshots confirm each of these by name, though none of them come with a stated price, performance figures, or details on how they are stored or maintained in game.

## Jason's personal pickup

One vehicle gets special treatment. Rockstar describes Jason's own truck as a well worn Vapid Ganado, a low riding pickup, and the Ultimate Edition adds a customised version of it called the Ganado Retro Build, with exclusive modifications Rockstar has not itemised in detail. The base Ganado and the Retro Build variant are both named directly by Rockstar, but only the Retro Build is confirmed as the Ultimate Edition bonus. Treat them as related rather than identical until more is shown.

## The pre-order bonus car

Separately from the Ultimate Edition, anyone who pre-orders either edition of the game before 20 November 2026 gets the Vintage Vice City Pack. Its centerpiece is the '55 Vapid Stanier Sedan, a 1955-styled sedan that comes with its own garage. The same pack adds access to the Ocean Beach area, outfits and hairstyles for Jason and Lucia, and a themed weapon pattern, none of which are vehicles but are worth knowing as context for why this particular car exists as a bonus rather than a standard pickup.

## What none of this tells you

None of these eight vehicles represent the everyday car pool players will actually drive around Leonida. Grand Theft Auto games have always filled their streets with dozens of ordinary cars, trucks, and bikes that players find parked or driving around rather than unlock through an edition. Rockstar has not published that list for GTA 6, and nobody should trust a leaked or "datamined" car list as fact before release. The game has not shipped, so there is no way to verify any of it independently. If a car is not one of the eight above, treat any claim about it as unconfirmed until Rockstar or the released game itself says otherwise.

## When this will change

The full vehicle roster becomes knowable only after Grand Theft Auto VI actually launches on 19 November 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S. Once that happens, this page will move from covering eight bonus vehicles to reflecting whatever Rockstar and verified post-launch sources confirm about the wider car list. Until then, this is every vehicle Rockstar itself has put a name to, and nothing beyond it should be treated as settled.
