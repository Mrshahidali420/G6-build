## What this page actually covers

Rockstar Games has not published a weapons list for Grand Theft Auto VI. There
is no page on the Rockstar Newswire, no page on the Rockstar store, and no
entry in any press kit that sets out what the player can carry. So this section
is not a weapons list. It is a record of the weapons that have been named in
public, and of who did the naming.

That difference matters more here than anywhere else on the site. Weapon names
are the easiest thing in a Grand Theft Auto game to guess wrong. A wiki can
carry a full armoury two years before release, built from earlier games and
from what people think they saw in a trailer. Some of this page is built exactly
that way, and every entry that is says so on its face.

## Where these names come from

The names on this page come from three places, and each entry says which.

One weapon is named by Rockstar itself. The Hawk and Little Morgan Revolver
appears in the Compare Editions contents on Rockstar's own store page, sitting
next to an item called Personalized Weapon Variants. That is a first-party
listing, so the name and the Hawk and Little manufacturer are settled.

The rest were named by IGN, in its Grand Theft Auto VI weapons list, read on 2
September 2026. IGN watched the Extended Look gameplay reveal and Rockstar's
published screenshots and wrote down the names shown on screen. That is good
reporting, and it is still reporting. Until Rockstar prints the same names, an
entry sourced to IGN stays marked as press reported on this site.

The third group is the largest, and it is the weakest. Those names come from
the GTA Fandom wiki, collected through the outlawdb dataset and released under
CC BY-SA 3.0. The wiki is written by fans, so every one of those entries is
marked community identified, sits at tier 3, and carries low confidence. Each
one links to the exact wiki page it came from. Read those pages as a record of
what the wiki says, not as a statement about the game.

## What the Ultimate Edition changes

Rockstar's store page lists Personalized Weapon Variants as part of the
Ultimate Edition. IGN has reported that this covers special versions of the
Morgan Revolver for both Jason and Lucia, and that Jason's Girardi ES9 and
Lucia's Klose K17 can be personalized the same way.

What personalizing a weapon actually does is not explained anywhere. Whether it
is a paint finish, a set of attachments, a stat change, or all three, Rockstar
has not said. So this site does not say either.

## The real-world comparisons

Several entries note a real firearm the weapon resembles. The Klose K17 is
reported to be based on the Glock 17, the Girardi ES9 on the Beretta 92FS, the
Moreland 850 on the Remington 870, and the Duke 556 on the AR-15 pattern.

Every one of those comparisons is IGN's reading of a picture. Rockstar has
never named a real firearm as a basis for anything in this game, and it almost
certainly never will, because Grand Theft Auto has always used invented
manufacturers instead. Treat the comparison as a way to picture the weapon, not
as a fact about it.

## What none of this tells you

No damage figure, no rate of fire, no magazine size, no reload speed, no price
and no shop location has been published for a single Grand Theft Auto VI
weapon. Nobody outside Rockstar has played the game. Any site showing you a
stat table today built it from Grand Theft Auto V and hoped you would not
check.

It is also not known how many weapons the finished game has, whether the names
seen in preview footage are final, or whether a weapon shown in a screenshot is
something the player can pick up at all.

## When this will change

The moment Rockstar publishes a weapons list, or names a weapon on the
Newswire, the matching entry here moves from press reported to confirmed and
keeps both sources, so you can still see who said it first. Until then this
page stays short on purpose.

Each entry below prints the outlet that named the weapon and the date the entry
was last checked. If you find something wrong, tell us and it gets fixed.
