## What this page actually covers

Rockstar Games has not published an animal list for Grand Theft Auto VI. There
is no species list on the Rockstar Newswire, none in the press kit, and none on
the Grand Theft Auto VI site. So this page is not a list of the animals in the
game. It is a record of the animals that have been named in public, and of who
named them.

Animals are visible in the trailers. Flamingos, alligators, dolphins and dogs
have all been seen. Seeing an animal in a trailer is not the same as having a
name, a behaviour, or a place on the map, and none of those things has been
published for any of them.

## Where these names come from

Every entry in this section comes from the GTA Fandom wiki, collected through
the outlawdb dataset and released under CC BY-SA 3.0. The wiki is written by
fans. It is not a Rockstar source, and it is not a press source.

That is why every animal on this page carries the same three marks:

- **Community identified**, not official and not press reported.
- **Tier 3**, the community tier.
- **Low confidence**.

Each entry also links to the exact wiki page it came from, so you can read the
same claim at its source and judge it yourself.

## What that means when you read an entry

Treat each page as a record of a claim, not a fact about the game. The animal
may appear in Grand Theft Auto VI exactly as described. It may appear with a
different name. It may not appear at all. A fan wiki carries entries built from
earlier Grand Theft Auto games and from what people think they saw in a
trailer, and there is no way to separate those from first-hand information
until the game releases.

## What is not known about any of them

For every animal listed here, all of the following are unpublished:

- Whether the animal can be hunted, killed or interacted with
- Where on the map it appears
- How it behaves
- Whether Rockstar uses this name in the finished game

## When this page changes

Grand Theft Auto VI has not released. When Rockstar publishes anything that
names an animal, that entry moves up to the official tier and its confidence
rises with it. Until then the whole section stays where it is, at low
confidence, with the wiki named on every page.
