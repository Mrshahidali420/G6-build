## The short answer

On 27 August 2026 Rockstar published a gameplay video called **Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look**. Rockstar says it was "captured entirely from in-game footage on the PlayStation 5". It ran about 27 minutes. It premiered on Netflix, then went to Rockstar's own YouTube channel about six hours later. There was no voice-over and there were no captions, so every reading of it below is either Rockstar's own words or a named outlet's report.

## Who the video showed

- **Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos**, the two playable leads, working through the Leonida underworld.
- **Boobie Ike**. Jason and Lucia do a drug pickup for him near the start.
- **Brian Heder**. He meets the pair over dinner, gives them a job, and warns Jason not to go deeper into crime.
- **Raul Bautista**. Jason and Lucia drive the getaway car after his robbery and are taken into his crew.
- **San4San**, a character wearing a "Righteous Slaughter: Hood Ops" shirt.
- A **livestreaming influencer** that Lucia takes by accident during a police chase.
- A **dog**, which can be petted.

## Where the video went

- **Leonida**, the fictional state based on Florida.
- **Vice City**, the main city and GTA's stand-in for Miami.
- **The Everglades**, crossed by fanboat.
- **Rivers**, used for kayaking, and open water for scuba diving and jet skiing.
- **Nightclubs** lit in neon.
- An opening party for the first American office of a Spanish language TV network, where the pair work private security.

## What the video drove

- An old convertible **Sentinel**, a car brand that goes back to the first Vice City.
- A **dirt bike**, used for racing.
- A **jet ski**, a **fanboat** and a **kayak**.

In one chase the player shoots out a pursuing car's tyres, and the car flips and hits the pavement.

## The brands and jokes spotted

- **Misfire Games**, the in-game studio behind "Righteous Slaughter: Hood Ops". That name is a callback to "Righteous Slaughter 7" in GTA 5.
- **Fleeca Bank**, as a sticker on Jason's fridge.
- A **"Prairie"** sandwich box meal TV advert, sold at "only 30,000 calories".

## The systems it showed

- **Switching between Jason and Lucia**, including in the middle of a chase, to drive or to shoot.
- A **minimap drawn like a real GPS** while driving.
- **Damage that carries**: shot tyres flip cars, cover breaks, and one character puts a police officer through a wall.
- **Bodies that change**. Eating and working out change the characters' weight and shape.
- **Clothing changes**, which WIRED reports were worked on with real fashion designers.
- Everyday actions: getting a drink from the fridge, robbing a store, skydiving, dirt bike racing, going to a club.
- **An underwater world** with manatees, dolphins and stingrays.
- **Heat that shows**. Lucia visibly sweats inside a club.
- **Phones and texting** between the two leads.

## What it did not show

- **No PC version** was announced.
- **No user made content tools**. WIRED says nothing in the video pointed at mod or mission creator tools.
- **No commentary and no captions**, so nothing in the video explained itself.
- **No unlock hour** for launch day. The date is still 19 November 2026 on PS5, Xbox Series X and Series S.

## Two extra facts from the same day

- IGN reports the full story runs about **80 hours**.
- Netflix went down under the demand at the premiere, then recovered. Twitch also had trouble at the same time.
