## The short answer

Rockstar announced the GTA 6 pre-order date and prices on 24 June 2026. Pre-orders opened the next day, 25 June 2026, with a Standard Edition and an Ultimate Edition.

## The official US prices

- Standard Edition: USD 79.99
- Ultimate Edition: USD 99.99

Rockstar confirmed both prices directly in its Newswire post. The Ultimate Edition adds exclusive vehicles, weapons, apparel and businesses, plus two extra side missions, on top of everything already in the Standard Edition.

## What the Ultimate Edition adds

The Standard Edition is the base game on its own. The PlayStation Store's product page lists the Ultimate Edition's extra content as exclusive vehicles, weapons, apparel and businesses, plus two extra side missions not available in the Standard Edition.

## Regional prices

Rockstar's Newswire post set the US price directly. Prices in other regions come from press reports tracking regional storefronts, not from Rockstar itself, so treat them as likely rather than fully confirmed until Rockstar states them directly.

| Region | Standard | Ultimate | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| United States | USD 79.99 | USD 99.99 | Confirmed by Rockstar |
| United Kingdom | GBP 69.99 | GBP 89.99 | Press reported |
| Europe | EUR 79.99 | EUR 99.99 | Press reported |
| India | Rs 5,999 | Rs 7,499 | Press reported, medium confidence |
| Canada | CAD 109.99 | CAD 139.99 | Press reported |
| Australia | AUD 129.95 | AUD 159.95 | Press reported |

The UK price comes from TechRadar's coverage of the UK pre-order page, not a Rockstar post. The India, Europe, Canada and Australia figures come from other outlets tracking local storefronts, and none of them are confirmed by Rockstar in an official post.

## Where to pre-order

Digital pre-orders are available through console storefronts, including the PlayStation Store, where the Ultimate Edition's extra content is listed on the product page linked above.

## What we still don't know

Rockstar has not published a PC price, since no PC version has been announced. There is also no confirmed age rating yet, so pricing tied to a rating in some regions could still change later.

## Quick summary

Rockstar set the US prices directly at USD 79.99 for Standard and USD 99.99 for Ultimate. Every other regional price in this article comes from press reports, not an official Rockstar figure. Pre-orders opened worldwide on 25 June 2026, one day after this announcement.
