## The short answer

GTA 6 pre-orders opened worldwide on 25 June 2026. You can pre-order the Standard Edition or the Ultimate Edition, and any pre-order placed before 20 November 2026 comes with a free bonus pack.

## The two editions

- Standard Edition: USD 79.99
- Ultimate Edition: USD 99.99

Rockstar says the Ultimate Edition adds exclusive vehicles, weapons, apparel and businesses, plus two extra side missions. Those extras sit on top of everything already in the Standard Edition, not instead of it.

## The free Vintage Vice City Pack

Anyone who places a pre-order before 20 November 2026 gets the Vintage Vice City Pack for free, on either edition. Rockstar's support page lists what is inside:

- A 1955 Vapid Stanier sedan
- A garage to keep it in
- Access to Ocean Beach
- Outfits and hairstyles for both Jason and Lucia
- A themed weapon pattern

This pack does not cost anything extra. It is Rockstar's reward for pre-ordering early, and the cut-off date is fixed at 20 November 2026.

## One free month of GTA+

A digital pre-order of either edition also comes with one free month of GTA+, Rockstar's subscription service. This bonus is tied to digital pre-orders specifically, according to the Newswire announcement.

## Preload and launch day

Press reports say preloading starts on 12 November 2026, one week before the 19 November 2026 launch on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. This detail comes from GameSpot's coverage, not from Rockstar directly, so treat it as a press report rather than a confirmed Rockstar statement.

## Physical copies and the download code

Do not expect a full game disc in a physical copy's box. GameSpot reports that physical copies of GTA 6 ship with a download code rather than the whole game on disc, so most of the install still happens over the internet.

## What is not confirmed yet

Rockstar has not announced a PC version, a PC release date, or a PC price. There is also no published age rating and no announced file size, so anyone planning storage space will have to wait for official numbers.
