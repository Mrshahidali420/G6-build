## IGN, 15 September 2026

[Travis Scott, Future, and Other Artists Begin Teasing GTA 6's Soundtrack](https://www.ign.com/articles/rockstar-games-begins-teasing-gta-6s-soundtrack-with-travis-scott-future-morgan-wallen-and-more)

The outlet's own summary:

> Rockstar Games has begun teasing the soundtrack for Grand Theft Auto 6 with new artwork from Future, Travis Scott, and other artists.

> Actor Stephen Root also confirmed his long speculated role as Brian Heder in GTA 6 earlier this week, though seemingly through a slip-up rather than a planned reveal.

> Many assumed that these were all teasing GTA 6 in some capacity, and that was confirmed once Future uploaded a picture of a sports car repping a Leonida license plate.

> Dre was prominently featured as a character in GTA Online, Phil Colins was a character in Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories, and a number of comedians like Ricky Gervais have appeared in the flesh in games like GTA 4, so there's precedent for them to appear as actual characters.
