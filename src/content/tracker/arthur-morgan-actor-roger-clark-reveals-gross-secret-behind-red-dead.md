summary: "Roger Clark, the award-winning actor behind Red Dead Redemption 2's iconic protagonist, Arthur Morgan, recently divulged a 'gross secret' regarding his performance of the character's persistent coughs due to tuberculosis. While challenging, Clark revealed it was 'not as bad as you'd think' thanks to several factors. Clark explained that having a five-year recording period for his voice-capture performance allowed for flexibility. He would often save particularly intense coughing scenes for the end of a recording day, when his voice was naturally hoarse from extensive work. Even more surprisingly, Clark admitted to leveraging his own real-life illnesses. Over the half-decade production, he, like anyone, caught colds, and he would use these opportunities to record 'pretty damn genuine' coughs, complete with 'a nice good bit of phlegm.' Clark's portrayal of Arthur Morgan earned him Best Performance at The Game Awards in 2018. Red Dead Redemption 2 itself has achieved monumental success, selling over 87 million copies globally, solidifying its place as one of the top five best-selling games of all time.
