summary: "The Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 open beta, initially set to end on September 1, has been extended by a full 12 hours. Players now have until September 2 at 10 PM PT / 1 AM ET to participate. To further incentivize participation and reward players, Activision is offering double progression XP and double weapon XP for the entire extended period. This provides a significant boost for those aiming to reach the level 30 cap and claim exclusive beta rewards, including a special weapon blueprint. Following the beta's conclusion, the next opportunity to play MW4 multiplayer will be at the game's official launch on October 23. For players who pre-order, the single-player campaign will be accessible a week earlier. Modern Warfare 4 is confirmed for release on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Switch 2, and PC, marking a return to Nintendo platforms and the first time the series has skipped last-gen consoles since 2013's Call of Duty: Ghosts. Interestingly, the launch of MW4 will precede the highly anticipated arrival of GTA 6 on November 19 by 34 days."
