summary: "Digital Foundry's Oliver Mackenzie and Richard Leadbetter have provided an in-depth analysis of the Grand Theft Auto 6 gameplay reveal, labeling it potentially the \"most impressive example of real-time game rendering\" they have ever witnessed. The experts lauded GTA 6's exceptional visual presentation, highlighting standout character rendering, including intricate hair physics and skin details for both protagonists and NPCs. The game's implementation of ray tracing also received praise for its strategic and impactful application, balanced with conventional rendering methods to optimize fidelity and performance.Regarding performance, Digital Foundry noted that while GTA 6 targets a 30 frames per second (FPS) experience, it appears to hit this target consistently, with rare dips observed across an analysis of over 48,000 frames. The preview footage was calculated to render at a native 1440p, then upscaled to 4K without modern upscaling techniques, which resulted in a slightly soft image that may be refined before launch.Crucially, Digital Foundry reaffirmed its prediction that Rockstar is unlikely to offer a 60fps performance mode for GTA 6 on current-generation consoles. This is attributed primarily to the CPU bottleneck presented by the consoles, which struggles with the extensive world simulation required for the game, making the goal of halving rendering time nearly impossible without a significantly more capable processor. While the PlayStation 5 Pro might offer improvements in ray-traced effects or resolution, 60fps is still not expected. A future PC release is anticipated to be the platform where 60fps might be showcased."
