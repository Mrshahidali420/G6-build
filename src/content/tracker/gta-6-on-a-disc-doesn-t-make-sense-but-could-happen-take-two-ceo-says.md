summary: "Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has offered insights into the distribution strategy for the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6, stating that while discs \"don't really make sense for the consumer\" in today's gaming landscape, the company hasn't entirely ruled out a physical release for the game down the line. Speaking to IGN, Zelnick highlighted that over 90% of Take-Two's current sales are digital, emphasizing that most modern games require an internet connection, making digital delivery the most efficient method. Initially, GTA 6 will not ship on a physical disc; instead, it will be available at retailers as a code in a box. However, Zelnick adopted an open-minded stance, noting, \"I wouldn't rule out anything at this stage of the game\" regarding potential future disc releases. The executive anticipates no negative financial impact from this digital-first approach, suggesting it could even boost Take-Two's revenue. He projects a future with near-universal digital game adoption, likening the eventual niche for physical games to the vinyl market in music. Zelnick clarified that the decision for a digital-only launch was based on what was \"right\" for GTA 6, rather than being influenced by Sony's announced end of physical disc support. Grand Theft Auto 6 is set to launch on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, priced at $80, which Take-Two defends as a fair value for the content offered."
