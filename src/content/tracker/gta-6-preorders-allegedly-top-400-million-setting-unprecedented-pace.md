summary: "A new report from third-party research firm Sensor Tower suggests that *Grand Theft Auto 6* has already amassed an astounding **$429 million in revenue** from preorders alone, selling 4.38 million copies by August 18. This figure, while **unconfirmed by Rockstar or Take-Two**, highlights the immense anticipation surrounding the highly awaited title. According to Sensor Tower's data, the sales mix heavily favors PlayStation 5, accounting for 77% of preorders, with Xbox Series X|S making up the remaining 23%. Preorders for the game, which began on June 25, offer a standard edition at $80 and an Ultimate Edition at $100, with the pricier Ultimate Edition reportedly proving more popular. Rockstar's parent company, Take-Two, has previously stated that *GTA 6*'s preorder numbers are \"unprecedented\" across the industry but has refrained from disclosing official figures, citing potential for them to be \"misleading.\" Industry experts anticipate that the majority of preorders will occur closer to the game's launch, with one analyst projecting up to 25 million preorders by release, potentially generating $2 billion in revenue—enough to cover development and most marketing costs. *GTA 6* is slated for release on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Its official marketing campaign is expected to commence with a trailer debut on Netflix on August 27. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick noted that despite the game's $80 standard price point (compared to GTA 5's $60 in 2013, which is about $85 today with inflation), the company aimed to keep it accessible for most players."
