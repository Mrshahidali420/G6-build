He chose not to comment on specific sales expectations but expressed immense excitement, stating, \"The news is great so far, we're incredibly excited. We couldn't be more excited than we are, and yet we are realistic and, at best, cautiously optimistic.\" These remarks come after Take-Two previously affirmed that GTA 6 has not been delayed and remains on track for its November 19 release date.
