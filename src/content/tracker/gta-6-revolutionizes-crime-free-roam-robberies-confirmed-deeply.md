summary: "Grand Theft Auto 6 is set to redefine its criminal underworld with a groundbreaking free-roam robbery system deeply intertwined with the main story progression. Players will no longer be solely reliant on linear missions to advance, as the game will sometimes require a specific amount of money before the narrative continues, empowering them to earn it through various illicit activities across Leonida. A significant innovation is the ability to rob *any* store, not just designated locations, adding a layer of organic interaction to the open world. These robberies will be dynamic, featuring security cameras, back entrances, safes requiring codes, and even intervening NPCs, ensuring no two heists are exactly alike. GTA 6 introduces a nuanced money system: banked money is secure, physical cash can be lost upon death, and stolen loot (like jewelry or drugs) must be offloaded to specialized fences. The game will feature a spectrum of robbery opportunities, from small shops to high-stakes jewelry stores, banks, and even gang compounds, with armored vans providing smaller, mobile targets. Larger opportunities won't always be marked on the map, encouraging exploration and interaction to discover them through tips or contacts. Players can also scope out known targets to see estimated payouts. Crucially, these free-roam criminal endeavors are designed to feel like an integral part of the main story, not a separate side activity. Money earned can be spent on a wide array of customization options for Jason and Lucia, including clothing, vehicles, garages, and even registering stolen high-end cars (with progression requirements). Rockstar explicitly aims to rectify GTA V's issue of players accumulating vast sums of money with little to spend it on, promising more meaningful financial sinks in GTA 6. While massive heists are confirmed to return, it's still uncertain if these will be open-world or strictly mission-based."
