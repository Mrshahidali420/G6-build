summary: "The recent 26-minute **GTA 6 Extended Look** trailer, a strategic collaboration between Netflix and Rockstar Games, has reportedly had a significant impact on Netflix's subscriber growth. According to Ampere Analysis, the trailer drove more than **100,000 new Netflix sign-ups** in the US alone within a single day. This makes the GTA 6 event the **third-biggest Netflix event for new subscriber sign-ups in 2026**, outranked only by the final season of *Stranger Things* and a major MMA fight. Beyond new subscribers, the trailer also successfully reactivated lapsed viewers. Ampere Analysis noted that over 5% of US Netflix members who hadn't engaged with the service in the preceding 30 days returned specifically to watch the Extended Look, a \"reactivation rate\" higher than any other content during that period. Louise Wooldridge from Ampere highlighted the mutual benefits, stating it demonstrated \"the pulling power of a major gaming event\" for Netflix to compete for gamer attention and bring back viewers, while opening GTA 6 up to audiences beyond its traditional core. The Extended Look itself provided an in-depth preview of Rockstar's upcoming title, showcasing various gameplay segments, cutscenes, and teasing new features. GTA 6 is scheduled to launch on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with reports suggesting US players may be among the last globally to access the game."
