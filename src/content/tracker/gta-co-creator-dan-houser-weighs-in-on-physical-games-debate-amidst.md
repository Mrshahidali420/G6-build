summary: "Rockstar Games co-founder and Grand Theft Auto co-creator, Dan Houser, has offered his thoughts on the ongoing debate surrounding physical game releases. In an interview with IGN, Houser expressed a somewhat detached personal view, stating, \"I don't know that I care.\" However, he quickly added a more consumer-centric stance: \"If people want that, I think companies should provide it.\" This commentary comes amidst significant industry shifts, most notably Sony's confirmation that it will cease PlayStation disc production by 2028, and the revelation that the highly anticipated GTA 6 will be a digital-exclusive title, with physical retail copies containing only download codes. Houser, who departed Rockstar in 2020 and now leads Absurd Ventures, acknowledged the practical benefits of digital distribution, particularly \"the ability to update and fix problems,\" which he believes has been \"really useful for the quality of games.\" Despite his pragmatic view on digital advantages, he also confessed, \"I love physical media.\" His current colleague at Absurd Ventures, former Rockstar veteran Lazlow Jones, echoed a similar sentiment, appreciating both the tactile experience of discs and the convenience of digital downloads. Meanwhile, Dan's brother, Sam Houser, continues to lead Rockstar Games as GTA 6's November release approaches. Absurd Ventures is currently developing multiple open-world action-adventure games, including one set in a sci-fi universe and another in a comedy satire world."
