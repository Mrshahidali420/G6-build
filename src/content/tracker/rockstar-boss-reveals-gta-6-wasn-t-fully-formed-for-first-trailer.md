summary: "Rockstar North boss Rob Nelson has revealed that *Grand Theft Auto VI* was not \"fully formed\" when its highly anticipated first trailer debuted in December 2023. In an interview with Trois Couleurs, Nelson explained that Tom Petty's 'Love is a Long Road,' featured in the trailer, acted as a crucial \"mission statement\" for the development team, providing a guiding light for the game's overall feel and direction. The trailer, he noted, represents \"the best of everything you’ve got at that point\" to help coalesce the vision. Nelson also shed light on the initial stages of development, detailing how one of the first steps after deciding on Vice City (Miami) as the setting was for a small team to travel there. They immersed themselves in the city, taking pictures and soaking up the atmosphere to inform the game's design. The article confirms that *GTA 6* is slated for release on November 19 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Furthermore, it highlights significant early success, with SensorTower data indicating the game has already sold 5 million copies via preorders, generating over $500 million in revenue. In a partnership with Sony, a *GTA 6*-themed DualSense controller is also on the way, set to launch on September 10 for $85."
