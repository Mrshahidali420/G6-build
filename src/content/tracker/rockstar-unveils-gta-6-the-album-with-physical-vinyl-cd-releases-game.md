summary: "Rockstar Games has officially pulled back the curtain on **Grand Theft Auto 6: The Album**, a sprawling 34-song collection set to immerse players in the world of the upcoming blockbuster. Partnering with Atlantic Records, the album boasts an impressive roster of original music from iconic artists such as Keith Richards, Yung Lean, Morgan Wallen, Rauw Alejandro, Travis Scott, and more.Despite the highly anticipated game itself being a digital-exclusive release, fans will be able to get their hands on physical copies of the soundtrack. 'The Album' will hit retailers on November 19, coinciding with GTA 6's launch, with multiple vinyl and CD versions available. Enthusiasts can choose from a standard vinyl for $50, a CD for $20, or a striking limited-edition vinyl for $125, featuring two transparent magenta discs filled with blue liquid.To whet appetites, Rockstar has already released six debut singles for streaming, including tracks from Yung Lean, Travis Scott, and Keith Richards. This announcement follows earlier teasers from various artists, building on the Grand Theft Auto series' long-standing tradition of featuring rich and diverse in-game radio stations, with rumors even suggesting DJ Khaled and Drake might host their own in GTA 6. The album's physical release offers a tangible piece of the GTA 6 experience, even as the game embraces its digital-only future on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S."
