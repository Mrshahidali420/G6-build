Zelnick emphasized that while AI can assist in asset creation or storyboarding, it does not guarantee a hit in an already saturated market. He highlighted that entertainment success stems from core creative value, not just efficient production, drawing comparisons to AI-generated music and PowerPoint presentations. Zelnick also clarified that while AI is used internally at Take-Two for cost and time efficiencies, Rockstar Games did not employ generative AI in the development of the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6, which is slated for a November 19, 2026 release on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S."
