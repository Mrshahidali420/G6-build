summary: "Rockstar Games' highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6 is set to launch in November for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with no PC version available at release. However, Take-Two Interactive CEO Strauss Zelnick's recent comments strongly suggest that a PC release is a matter of 'when, not if.' Speaking at a virtual shareholder meeting, Zelnick stated that Take-Two aims to release games 'for any platforms for which there is a meaningful audience,' adding that 'the PC is becoming more and more important to our company over time.' He highlighted a significant shift in PC sales, noting that for a 'big title,' PC can now account for 45-50% of total sales, a dramatic increase from the approximately 5% seen for titles like NBA 2K in 2007. Historically, Rockstar titles like Grand Theft Auto 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 launched on consoles first before making their way to PC, typically after a waiting period of 12 to 18 months. This precedent suggests PC players should anticipate a wait of at least a year for GTA 6. The delay is not attributed to a co-marketing deal with Sony, but rather Rockstar's strategy of 'serving the core [audience]' (console players) first and potentially creating a 'second selling cycle' for the game on PC. A former Rockstar developer, Mike York, also echoed this sentiment, stating the company tends to 'prioritize what sells' initially."
